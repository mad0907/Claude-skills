How to use

Choose the way that suits you

Option 1 works for everyone, including free Claude accounts, and takes about a minute. You can move to Options 2 or 3 later.

1

Copy and paste

Easiest · any Claude plan · 1 minute

  1. Open any skill in the Library (or use the Playground).
  2. Click Copy & open Claude. The skill and your request are copied together.
  3. In Claude, paste (Ctrl/⌘+V) and press Enter.

Nothing to install. You can also paste into any other AI assistant.

2

Add a skill to Claude

Pro, Max, Team & Enterprise · 2 minutes

  1. Click Download skill (.zip) on any skill. Do not unzip it.
  2. In Claude, go to Settings → Capabilities, make sure code execution is on, then find Skills.
  3. Click Upload skill, choose the .zip, and switch it on.

From then on, Claude uses the skill automatically whenever your request matches it.

3

Install all 83 at once

Claude desktop app or Claude Code · 3 minutes

  1. In the Claude desktop app, open Customize → Plugins.
  2. Next to Personal plugins, click + → Add marketplace and enter mad0907/Claude-skills.
  3. Click Browse plugins, find moons2star-consulting and click Install.
Using Claude Code (terminal)? 
/plugin marketplace add mad0907/Claude-skills
/plugin install moons2star-consulting@moons2star

Then type /moons2star-consulting: to see every skill.

Menu names in Claude can change. If something looks different, see Claude's help centre: Using Skills in Claude.

Questions

Plain-English FAQ

What is a "skill"?

A skill is a written set of instructions that teaches Claude how to do one kind of task well, like a senior colleague's checklist. For example, Board Paper tells Claude the sections a board expects, the order, the quality checks and the common mistakes. Claude then follows that method on your problem.

What is a "plugin"?

A plugin is a bundle of skills you install in one go. This plugin, moons2star-consulting, contains all 83 skills. Once it's installed, Claude picks the right skill from your request, or you can call one by name.

Do I need GitHub or any technical knowledge?

No. GitHub is simply where the files are stored. Use Option 1 (copy and paste) or Option 2 (upload a .zip in Claude's settings). Neither needs GitHub, coding or a terminal.

Which Claude plan do I need?

Copy and paste works on any plan, including free. Uploading skills is available on paid plans (Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise) with code execution turned on. On Team and Enterprise, an admin may need to enable Skills first. Plans and menus change, so check Claude's help centre for the latest.

Is my data safe?

This website stores nothing and sends nothing. The Playground runs entirely in your browser. What you paste into Claude is handled under your Claude account's terms and your organisation's settings. Follow your company's policy, and anonymise confidential or personal data.

What are the limitations? (Please read)

These skills are decision support, not professional advice. They give Claude a structured method; they are not legal, financial, investment, tax, accounting, regulatory or medical advice, and they don't replace qualified advisers or your organisation's approval processes. AI can produce confident but wrong facts, numbers or legal references, so verify anything you will rely on, sign or publish. Laws, standards and dates mentioned (for example SEBI/BRSR, the EU AI Act, India's DPDP Rules) reflect research as of November 2026 and change often, so confirm against official sources. Output is only as good as the context you provide. Don't share confidential, personal or price-sensitive data unless your policies allow it. The toolkit is provided "as is", without warranty; using it creates no adviser-client relationship, and the author accepts no liability for decisions made with it.

What does "In-depth" mean?

In-depth skills include extra reference files: a full worked example, detailed criteria and checklists, and the standards and regulations they draw on, with dates. When you copy one, the reference files are included in the prompt automatically. All 83 skills are built to this depth, with the same structure: the senior standard, diagnosis, method, judgement calls, red flags, variants by context, an output template and a quality check.

Will the answers always be right?

No AI output should be used unchecked. The skills tell Claude never to invent facts, to label estimates and to flag legal or regulatory points for verification. You remain the reviewer: check numbers, sources and anything you'll put your name to.
