# Worked example (illustrative): restructuring an email to the CEO

**Original draft (chronological):**
"Over the last month the team reviewed the Brazil launch plan. We met the regulator's consultants, who mentioned that approval timelines have been extended. We also heard that our distributor may be acquired. Finance re-ran the numbers… We therefore think it might be better to consider pausing."

**Governing thought:** pause the Brazil launch for six months, to protect ₹15 Cr of launch investment from avoidable risk.

```
Pause the Brazil launch for six months
├─ 1. Regulatory approval is likely to slip to Q3 (not Q1)
│   ├─ Consultants report the backlog doubled since the new rules
│   └─ Two peers' approvals took 9-11 months this year
├─ 2. Our distributor is being acquired, so terms may change
│   ├─ Acquisition announced 3 Oct; closing expected Q2
│   └─ The acquirer has its own competing brand
└─ 3. A pause costs < ₹2 Cr vs ₹15 Cr at risk
    ├─ Holding costs: team and storage ₹1.6 Cr
    └─ Launching without approval or distribution risks the full launch spend
```

**Logic:** inductive (three independent reasons). **Order:** importance.

**Rewritten email:**

> **Subject:** Recommend pausing the Brazil launch for six months (decision by Friday)
>
> I recommend we pause the Brazil launch for six months. It protects the ₹15 Cr launch investment for under ₹2 Cr in holding costs.
>
> - **Approval will likely slip to Q3:** the regulator's backlog has doubled, and two peers waited 9-11 months.
> - **Our distributor is being acquired:** closing is expected in Q2, and the acquirer has a competing brand, so our terms may change.
> - **The cost of pausing is small:** ₹1.6 Cr in holding costs, vs ₹15 Cr at risk.
>
> If you agree, we'll inform the Brazil team Monday and return in April with a revised plan, including a backup distributor.
