# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Pyramid-style, top-down structuring:** a governing thought supported by grouped, MECE arguments with inductive or deductive logic. A standard executive-writing method in consulting and business-school communication training.
- **"Bottom line up front" (BLUF):** government and military writing guidance.
- **Plain-language standards:** ISO 24495-1:2023 (plain language) and public-sector plain-language guidelines.
- **Logic:** inductive vs deductive reasoning, from introductory logic and critical-thinking curricula.
