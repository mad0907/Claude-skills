# Sources (public standards and widely taught practice; verify current versions)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Spreadsheet modelling standards:** publicly available, industry-developed best-practice standards for financial models: separation of inputs, calculations and outputs; consistency; transparency; integrity checks. Several free standards exist and are widely used by banks, project-finance practitioners and model auditors.
- **Spreadsheet risk research:** academic and practitioner research consistently finds error rates in a high share of operational spreadsheets, which supports mandatory checks and peer review.
- **Three-statement linkages, DCF valuation, working-capital days, project-finance metrics (DSCR, LLCR):** as taught in standard corporate-finance and financial-modelling curricula.
- **Accounting:** Ind AS / IFRS (e.g. Ind AS 115 revenue; Ind AS 116 leases; Ind AS 16 PP&E) for how items should appear in statements. Confirm with Finance and auditors.
