# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **MECE principle:** a core structured-thinking principle in business problem solving and executive communication.
- **Classification and taxonomy design:** mutually exclusive categories with explicit inclusion rules, from information science and statistics (e.g. industry classification systems).
- **Revenue and profit decomposition:** management-accounting identities used as the most robust MECE logic.
- **Risk taxonomies:** standard enterprise-risk category structures (e.g. per ISO 31000-aligned frameworks) with primary-category rules.
