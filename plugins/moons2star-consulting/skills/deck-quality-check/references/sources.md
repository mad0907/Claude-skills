# Sources (widely taught concepts; verify regulatory specifics)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Structured communication review:** storyline (answer-first), horizontal and vertical logic, MECE groupings (see `pyramid-structure`, `action-titles`).
- **Graphical integrity:** honest scales and sourcing (see `chart-selection`).
- **India regulatory considerations for listed companies:** SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 on UPSI; SEBI LODR on disclosures. Investor materials must not selectively disclose price-sensitive information (verify with the Company Secretary).
- **Data protection:** DPDP Act 2023 (personal data in documents shared internally and externally).
- **Review practice:** prioritised feedback (must, should, nice) common in quality-assurance and peer-review processes.
