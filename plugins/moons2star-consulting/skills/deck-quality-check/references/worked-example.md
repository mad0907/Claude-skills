# Worked example (illustrative; 18-slide board deck proposing an acquisition)

**Verdict:** Ready after must-fixes. The storyline is strong, but two numbers don't tie and the risk section is thin for a board.

**Storyline (titles only):** answer-first ✓. Slides 9-11 repeat the synergy argument. Merge them into one slide and move the detail to the appendix. The ask on slide 18 lacks the resolution wording.

| Priority | Slide | Issue | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Must-fix | 3 vs 12 | Target EBITDA shown as ₹84 Cr (slide 3) and ₹87 Cr (slide 12) | Directors will spot it and question every number | Use ₹84 Cr (audited FY26); slide 12 used management accounts. Footnote the difference |
| Must-fix | 7 | Title: "Synergies of ₹40 Cr are secure"; the chart shows ₹40 Cr *identified*, with ₹14 Cr at low confidence | Overclaim; the board will discount it | Title: "₹40 Cr of synergies identified; ₹26 Cr high-confidence" |
| Must-fix | 15 | Risks list has no mitigations or owners | The board cannot assess residual risk | Add mitigations, owners and residual ratings (see `risk-assessment`) |
| Must-fix | 18 | No resolution or approval limit | The board can't approve a vague ask | Add a draft resolution with price cap and delegation |
| Should-fix | 9-11 | Repetitive synergy slides | Loses attention | Merge into one slide |
| Should-fix | 5 | Market-share chart axis starts at 20% | Exaggerates the share gain | Start at 0, or use a table |
| Nice-to-have | All | Mixed "Rs" / "₹" and "crore" / "Cr" | Polish | Standardise on ₹ Cr |

**Number tie-out (extract):**
| Metric | Values found | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purchase price | ₹1,150 Cr (2, 16, 18) | Term sheet | ✓ |
| Target EBITDA | ₹84 (3), ₹87 (12) | Audited FY26: ₹84 | ✗ fix slide 12 |
| EV/EBITDA | 13.7x (2) | 1,150 ÷ 84 = 13.7 | ✓ |

**Anticipated tough questions:**
- "What do we pay for synergies we may not get?" → add a premium-vs-synergy slide.
- "Who leads the integration?" → name the leader on slide 17.

**Compliance:** the deck contains UPSI. Mark it confidential and restrict circulation. Remove the target employee names on slide 14 (personal data).
