# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Inference for proportions:** margin of error, two-proportion z-tests, chi-square tests, confidence intervals, as taught in introductory statistics.
- **Weighting:** post-stratification and raking to known population margins; effective sample size (Kish approximation (Σw)² ÷ Σw²).
- **Data quality:** speeders, straight-lining, attention checks; the total-survey-error framework.
- **Multiple comparisons:** the false-discovery risk when running many tests; pre-specification.
- **Driver analysis:** correlation and regression; importance-performance analysis; association vs causation.
- **Standards:** ISO 20252; market-research codes of conduct; data protection laws (DPDP Act 2023 in India).
