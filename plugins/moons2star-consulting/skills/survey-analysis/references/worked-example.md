# Worked example (illustrative; the B2B logistics software customer survey)

**Field:**
- 9,600 contacts invited; 1,262 completes.
- 62 removed (31 speeders, 19 straight-liners, 12 failed attention checks), leaving **n = 1,200 clean**.
- Response rate 12.5% on clean completes.

**Representativeness:** the sample is 70% SME (840) and 30% enterprise (360), but the customer base is 85% SME / 15% enterprise. Results are weighted to the true mix (SME weight 1.21, enterprise weight 0.50).
- **Effective n ≈ 1,084**, so the margin of error is about **±3.0 points** overall.
- Per segment: SME ±3.4, enterprise ±5.2 (at 50%).

**Bottom line:**
- Satisfaction is moderate (60% satisfied, weighted).
- Enterprise customers are markedly less satisfied, driven by onboarding and support response time.
- Investment should go to onboarding and support capacity before a mobile app.
- **Confidence:** high on the direction; medium on driver magnitudes.

| Finding | Evidence | Significant? | Confidence | So what |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 60% of customers are satisfied (top-2 box), weighted; the unweighted figure would show 58% | SME 62% (n=840); enterprise 48% (n=360) | — | High | Report the weighted figure; the unweighted sample understates SMEs |
| Enterprise satisfaction is 14 points below SME | 48% vs 62% | Yes (z ≈ 4.5) | High | Enterprise retention plan needed; these are the highest-value accounts |
| North-region customers are more satisfied than South | 48% (n=400) vs 38% (n=500) | Yes (z ≈ 3.0) | Medium | Investigate South support coverage (local language, hours) |
| Mid-size (250-999) vs large (1,000+) enterprise satisfaction differs | 31% vs 26% (n=150 each) | **No** (z ≈ 0.96) | Low | Not detectable with this sample; don't act on it |
| Field users need mobile access, but it's not a top driver | 41% use the product away from a desk weekly+; mobile ranked 4th of 6 as the "one improvement" | — | Medium | Phase the mobile app after onboarding and support fixes |

**Key distribution:** value for money is polarised in enterprise (34% good or very good, 38% poor or very poor) but not in SME. The average alone (3.0) hides this.

**Driver analysis (correlation with overall satisfaction; association, not causation):**
- Onboarding r = 0.52; support response time r = 0.47; ease of use r = 0.44; reporting r = 0.31; integrations r = 0.28; price r = 0.21.
- **Performance is lowest on** onboarding (41% satisfied) and support response time (44%). These are high-association, low-performance, so they are priorities.

**Open-text themes (Q4, n = 1,036 answered):**
- Slow support replies: 212.
- Hard initial set-up: 174.
- Missing reports: 96.
- Price: 71.

**Limitations:**
- Customers only (no lost customers).
- Self-reported satisfaction.
- Fieldwork in the quarter-end peak may depress support scores.
- **Validate:** link responses to churn data next quarter.
