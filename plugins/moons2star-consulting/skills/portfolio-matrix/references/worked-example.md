# Worked example (illustrative; fictional diversified Indian manufacturer, 6 business units; WACC 12%)

**Attractiveness weights:** market growth 25%, profit pool / margins 25%, competitive intensity 20%, cyclicality 15%, ESG and regulatory risk 15%.
**Strength weights:** relative share 30%, relative cost 25%, capabilities 25%, customer access 20%.

| Unit | Revenue (₹ Cr) | Growth | EBITDA % | ROCE | Attract. | Strength | Box | Role | Best owner? | Capital shift (3-yr capex) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Specialty polymers | 1,200 | 14% | 21% | 24% | 4.2 | 4.0 | High-High | **Invest to grow** | Us | ₹600 → ₹900 Cr |
| Engineering plastics compounds | 900 | 11% | 15% | 16% | 3.8 | 2.9 | High-Med | **Selectively invest** (auto EV segment only) | Us | ₹250 → ₹300 Cr |
| Industrial adhesives | 650 | 8% | 17% | 19% | 3.1 | 3.4 | Med-Med | **Maintain / earn** | Us | ₹120 → ₹100 Cr |
| Commodity PVC pipes | 1,500 | 6% | 9% | 11% | 2.3 | 3.2 | Low-Med | **Harvest** | Possibly a scale player | ₹300 → ₹120 Cr |
| Solar encapsulants | 180 | 30% | 6% | 5% | 4.0 | 1.8 | High-Low | **Fix** (24 months: cost parity or partner) | Unclear | ₹150 → ₹80 Cr (conditional) |
| Textile auxiliaries | 220 | 3% | 7% | 8% | 1.9 | 1.7 | Low-Low | **Divest** | A focused textile-chemicals player | ₹40 → ₹10 Cr |

**Portfolio balance:**
- Pipes and adhesives generate ~60% of free cash flow, which funds polymers and EV compounds.
- After reallocation, the share of capex going to the two growth-role units (invest and selectively invest) rises from 58% (₹850 of ₹1,460 Cr) to 79% (₹1,200 of ₹1,510 Cr).
- Commodity-cycle exposure is reduced.

**Conditions:**
- **Solar encapsulants:** reach cost within 5% of import landed cost by Q4 FY28, or form a JV / exit.
- **Pipes:** maximise cash; review sale options if a buyer offers ≥8x EBITDA.
- **Textile auxiliaries:** sale process in FY27; a transitional supply agreement for shared customers (₹30 Cr of revenue overlaps with adhesives).

**Interdependency:** pipes share a plant and utilities with adhesives. A separation-cost study is needed before any pipes sale.
