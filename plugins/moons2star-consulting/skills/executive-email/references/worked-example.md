# Worked examples (illustrative)

## 1. A decision request to the CFO

**Before (draft):**
- **Subject:** Pilot update
- **Body:** a 420-word message, opening with the pilot history; the ask appears in paragraph 5.

**After:**

**Subject:** Decision by Thu 14 Nov: extend the AI contract-review pilot (₹2.4 Cr) or stop

Anil, I need your approval by Thursday to extend the contract-review pilot by 6 months at ₹2.4 Cr. It cut review time by 39% with no rise in missed clauses, and the vendor's discounted rate expires on Friday.

- **Results (12 weeks, 1,840 contracts):** review time 5.1 → 3.1 hours; error rate flat at 1.8%; lawyer satisfaction 4.2 / 5.
- **Extension scope:** add procurement contracts; build the integration with our contract system.
- **Risk:** vendor lock-in. The extension includes a data-export clause.

**Options:**
- **A.** Extend 6 months at ₹2.4 Cr (recommended).
- **B.** Pause and re-tender. That loses the 20% discount and adds about 3 months.
- **C.** Stop. The ~₹6 Cr a year of capacity benefit is not realised.

**Next steps:**
- With your approval, Procurement signs by Friday.
- I'll report results monthly in the ops review.

Thanks, Priya

*(about 150 words)*

## 2. Bad news to the CEO

**Subject:** Plant 2 line stoppage: 4-day delay to the festive shipment; recovery plan attached

Rakesh, Line 3 at Plant 2 is down after a gearbox failure. Festive shipments to North dealers will be about 4 days late, putting roughly ₹18 Cr of October revenue at risk.

- **Cause:** a bearing failure. The spare was not in stock because of a supplier delay. Investigation underway.
- **Recovery:** a replacement arrives Wednesday; overtime on Lines 1-2; 30% of volume diverted to Plant 1. Expected recovery is about ₹13 Cr of the ₹18 Cr within October.
- **Dealer communication:** the sales team calls the top 50 dealers today.

**No decision is needed now.** I'll update you Wednesday evening.

If you'd like to speak to the plant head directly, I'll set up a call.

— Meera
