# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Bottom line up front (BLUF):** a communication convention from military and government writing, widely taught in business-writing courses.
- **Plain-language principles:** short sentences, active voice, front-loaded meaning (plain-language guidelines used by governments).
- **Answer-first structures:** see the `pyramid-structure` and `scqa-storyline` skills.
- **Records and privilege:** emails are discoverable records; legal privilege depends on the involvement of counsel and the purpose, not on labels alone. Seek legal advice.
- **Disclosure:** listed companies must avoid selective disclosure of price-sensitive information (SEBI PIT Regulations in India).
