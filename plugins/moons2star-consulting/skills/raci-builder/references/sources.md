# Sources (public standards and widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Responsibility assignment matrices (RACI and variants):** standard project- and process-management tools (e.g. ISO 21502:2020 roles and responsibilities; professional project-management standards).
- **Decision-rights frameworks:** recommend, agree, input, decide and perform role definitions, widely used in organisation design (generic terms).
- **Internal control:** segregation of duties and authorisation, as in internal-control frameworks (e.g. the COSO Internal Control - Integrated Framework); Companies Act 2013 s.134(5)(e) internal financial controls in India.
- **AI governance roles:** NIST AI RMF GOVERN; ISO/IEC 42001 roles, responsibilities and authorities.
