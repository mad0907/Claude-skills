# Sources (widely taught methods)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Multi-criteria decision analysis (MCDA):** weighted scoring with anchored scales; public appraisal guidance (e.g. UK HM Treasury *Green Book*: options appraisal, long-list to short-list, do-minimum baseline).
- **Decision quality principles:** an appropriate frame, creative alternatives, reliable information, clear values and trade-offs, sound reasoning, and commitment to action. Widely taught in decision-analysis curricula.
- **Real options and reversibility:** preferring staged, reversible commitments under high uncertainty.
- **Cognitive biases in strategic decisions:** anchoring, sunk cost, confirmation, overconfidence; debiasing practices such as pre-mortems and red-teaming.
- **Decision records:** documenting rationale and dissent, consistent with board minute-taking practice.
