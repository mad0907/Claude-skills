# Worked example (illustrative; ₹120 Cr warehouse automation for a consumer-goods distributor; hurdle rate 12%; 7-year horizon)

**Recommendation:** approve, with a gate. The base case is value-positive, but the case is highly sensitive to a delay in go-live, so a phased go-live and vendor delay penalties are conditions.

**vs do-nothing:** without automation, volume growth needs ~₹24 Cr a year of extra labour and leased space by year 3 (the do-nothing is not flat).

**Incremental cash flows (₹ Cr):**

| Year | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cash flow | −120 | 18 | 32 | 36 | 36 | 36 | 36 | 36 |
| Cumulative | −120 | −102 | −70 | −34 | 2 | 38 | 74 | 110 |

Benefits are net of run costs, and include working-capital release from faster order processing.

| Metric | Base | Downside (benefits −25%, capex +15%) | 1-year delay in benefits |
|---|---|---|---|
| NPV @ 12% | **+₹25.0 Cr** | −₹29.2 Cr | −₹5.0 Cr |
| IRR | **17.8%** | 5.5% | 10.9% |
| Payback | **~3.9 years** | ~5.7 years | ~4.9 years |

**Value drivers:**
- Labour productivity: picks per hour 110 → 260.
- Error-rate reduction: 1.2% → 0.3%.
- Avoided space.
- Throughput for growth.

**Sensitivity:**
- **Switching value:** benefits can fall about **17%** before NPV turns negative.
- A one-year delay alone makes NPV negative. **Schedule is the critical risk.**

**Risks and mitigations:**
- **Integration with the warehouse system and delay:** fixed-price contract with delay liquidated damages; a pilot zone first.
- **Volume shortfall:** modular automation, sized for 3-year volume.
- **Workforce transition:** redeploy 60% of affected staff to new sites; agree with the union early.

**Staging:**
- **Phase 1 (₹70 Cr):** 2 of 4 zones, with go-live by month 10.
- **Gate:** productivity ≥220 picks per hour and system stability for 8 weeks.
- **Phase 2 (₹50 Cr):** the remaining zones.

**The ask:**
- Approve ₹120 Cr in total, with Phase 2 release subject to the gate.
- **Conditions:** delay penalties in the vendor contract; a workforce plan agreed before signing.
- **KPIs:** picks per hour, error rate, cost per order.
- Quarterly reviews.

**Benefits owner:** Head of Supply Chain; tracked via `benefits-tracker`, with Finance validating.
