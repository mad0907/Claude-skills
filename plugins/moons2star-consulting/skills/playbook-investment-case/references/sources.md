# Sources (widely taught concepts and public standards)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Capital budgeting:** NPV, IRR, payback, incremental cash flows, working capital, terminal value, cost of capital, as taught in corporate-finance curricula.
- **Uncertainty analysis:** sensitivity, switching values, scenarios, real options and staging.
- **Optimism bias and reference-class forecasting:** base rates for cost overruns and benefit shortfalls in large projects.
- **Public-sector appraisal:** social cost-benefit analysis and value-for-money principles in government appraisal guidance.
- **Accounting context:** Ind AS 16 (property, plant and equipment) and Ind AS 36 (impairment) where relevant; verify with Finance.
