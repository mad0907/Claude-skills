# Sources, obligations and dates (as researched November 2026; verify before relying)

## Frameworks and standards
- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)**, Jan 2023: functions GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE. **NIST AI 600-1 Generative AI Profile**, Jul 2024.
- **ISO/IEC 42001:2023:** AI management system (certifiable). **ISO/IEC 23894:2023:** AI risk management guidance. **ISO/IEC 42005:2025:** AI system impact assessment. **ISO 31000:2018:** risk management.
- **OECD AI Principles** (2019, updated May 2024).
- **OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (2025)** and OWASP guidance on agentic AI threats.

## European Union: AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, as amended by the Digital Omnibus (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744)
- In force 1 Aug 2024. Prohibited practices and AI-literacy duty applied from 2 Feb 2025. General-purpose AI model obligations from 2 Aug 2025.
- Omnibus changes (reported July 2026, check the Official Journal text):
  - **Annex III high-risk** obligations moved to **2 Dec 2027** (from 2 Aug 2026).
  - **Annex I** (AI in regulated products) high-risk obligations moved to **2 Aug 2028**.
  - Systems for public authorities have until **2 Aug 2030**.
  - **Art. 50(2)** synthetic-content marking: systems already on the market before 2 Aug 2026 have until **2 Dec 2026**.
  - New Art. 5 prohibitions (e.g. non-consensual intimate imagery / "nudifier" apps, CSAM) apply from **2 Dec 2026**.
- Applies extraterritorially where AI output is used in the EU. Indian firms serving EU clients or EU persons may be in scope as providers or deployers.

## India
- **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** and **DPDP Rules, 2025** (G.S.R. 846(E), notified 13 Nov 2025):
  - Data Protection Board provisions from 13 Nov 2025.
  - Consent-manager rules from 13 Nov 2026.
  - Most data-fiduciary obligations (notice, consent, rights, security safeguards, breach notice, children's data, cross-border) from **13 May 2027**.
  - Breach reporting to the Board includes a detailed report within 72 hours.
  - Significant Data Fiduciaries must verify that algorithmic software does not pose risks to data principals' rights (Rule 13).
  - Penalties under the Act's Schedule (up to ₹250 crore for certain failures; verify).
- **MeitY: India AI Governance Guidelines** (released Nov 2025, under the IndiaAI Mission): principle-based, no standalone AI law; relies on existing laws, sandboxes, an India-specific risk classification, and new institutions (an AI Governance Group, an AI Safety Institute). Principles include Trust, People First, Innovation over Restraint, Fairness and Equity, Accountability, Understandable by Design, and Safety, Resilience and Sustainability.
- **RBI: FREE-AI Committee report** (Framework for Responsible and Ethical Enablement of AI, 13 Aug 2025): seven "sutras"; recommendations include board-approved AI policies, AI inventories, audit, incident reporting and AI disclosures in annual reports for regulated entities. Check for any subsequent RBI directions.
- **SEBI:** consultation (2025) on guidelines for responsible AI/ML use by market participants; check for a final circular.
- **CERT-In Directions** (Apr 2022): cyber-incident reporting, relevant where AI incidents are security incidents.
- **IRDAI / sector rules:** check outsourcing, IT and cyber, and any AI-specific guidance.

## Other jurisdictions to check where relevant
- **UK:** principles-based, regulator-led approach; ICO guidance on AI and data protection.
- **US:** sector regulators; state laws (e.g. Colorado AI Act, whose effective date was deferred; check); NYC Local Law 144 (automated employment decision tools: bias audits).
- **Singapore:** Model AI Governance Framework (including for GenAI); AI Verify.

**Always confirm:** current consolidated text, effective dates, and whether the organisation is a provider, deployer, importer or distributor (EU), or a significant data fiduciary (India).
