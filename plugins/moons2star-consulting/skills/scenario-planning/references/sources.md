# Sources (widely taught methods and public frameworks)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Scenario planning:** the established intuitive-logics method (focal question, driving forces, critical uncertainties, 2×2 matrix, narratives, implications, signposts), taught in strategy and foresight curricula.
- **Strategic foresight toolkits** published by governments and international organisations (e.g. OECD strategic foresight resources; UK Government Office for Science futures toolkit).
- **Climate scenarios for disclosure:** IFRS S2 climate-related scenario analysis requirements; publicly available climate scenario sets from central-bank and international networks. Use current versions.
- **Real options and robust decision-making under deep uncertainty:** no-regret moves, options and hedges.
- **ISO 31000:2018** and **ISO 31010:2019:** scenario analysis as a risk-assessment technique.
