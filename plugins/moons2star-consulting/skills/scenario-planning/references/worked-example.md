# Worked example (illustrative; fictional Indian IT services firm, $2B revenue)

**Focal question:** what mix of capabilities, delivery model and pricing should we commit to by 2030, given GenAI and agentic AI's impact on effort-based services?

**Predetermined trends:** clients will keep demanding AI-driven productivity gains; the talent pool keeps growing; cloud and data modernisation continue; data-sovereignty rules tighten in key markets.

**Critical uncertainties:**
- **A. Speed of AI productivity in delivery:** incremental (10-20% by 2030) ↔ step-change (40-60% by 2030).
- **B. Client commercial model:** effort and time-and-materials persist ↔ outcome and platform-based pricing dominates.

Independence check: fast AI could coexist with sticky T&M contracts (client procurement inertia), so the axes are independent.

| | B: effort-based persists | B: outcome-based dominates |
|---|---|---|
| **A: incremental** | **"Familiar Ground":** slow erosion of pricing; utilisation the key lever | **"Outcome Pioneers":** early movers win with IP-led outcomes; others commoditise |
| **A: step-change** | **"Deflation Trap":** clients demand 30-40% price cuts on the same effort contracts | **"Platform Economy":** value captured by firms with AI platforms, domain IP and agent operations |

| Variable (2030) | Familiar Ground | Outcome Pioneers | Deflation Trap | Platform Economy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue per delivery employee | +8% | +25% | −10% | +45% |
| Share of revenue from outcome / platform deals | 10% | 35% | 15% | 55% |
| Headcount vs 2026 | +15% | +5% | −10% | −20% |
| EBIT margin (industry) | 19% | 21% | 14% | 24% (leaders), 12% (laggards) |

**Stress test of the current strategy** (scale headcount, add AI as tools):
- Works in Familiar Ground.
- Struggles in Outcome Pioneers.
- **Fails** in Deflation Trap and Platform Economy.

**Moves:**
- **No-regret:**
  - Embed AI in all delivery and share the gains selectively.
  - Build 2-3 vertical IP assets (e.g. claims, KYC, reconciliation agents).
  - Retrain 30% of staff for AI-augmented roles.
  - Agent-operations capability with governance.
- **Options:**
  - Outcome-priced pilots with 5 anchor clients.
  - Acquire a small vertical AI platform.
  - A joint venture with a data-sovereign cloud provider in one region.
- **Hedges:**
  - Contract clauses sharing productivity gains.
  - Variable workforce models.
  - Diversify into regulated-industry managed services where trust and compliance matter.

**Signposts:**
| Indicator | Threshold | Points to | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Measured delivery productivity gain in our AI-enabled programmes | >30% sustained over 4 quarters | Step-change | Accelerate platform build; re-plan hiring | CTO |
| Share of new deals (TCV) priced on outcomes | >25% for 2 quarters | Outcome dominance | Scale outcome pricing; adjust risk policy | CRO / CFO |
| Average price-per-FTE on renewals | <−8% YoY | Deflation Trap | Trigger cost restructuring plan B | COO |

**Review:** signposts quarterly at ExCo; scenarios refreshed annually at the board strategy day.
