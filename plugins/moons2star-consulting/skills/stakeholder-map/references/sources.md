# Sources (widely taught concepts and public standards)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Stakeholder analysis:** power-interest (influence-impact) grids and salience concepts, from project-management standards and strategic-management curricula.
- **ISO 21502:2020** (project, programme and portfolio management: guidance on project management), stakeholder engagement sections.
- **Organisational network analysis:** identifying informal influence and brokers.
- **Change-management practice:** sponsorship, coalition building and resistance management (see the `change-plan` skill).
- **Employment and consultation law:** for affected employees, follow applicable labour law and consultation requirements (verify with HR and counsel).
