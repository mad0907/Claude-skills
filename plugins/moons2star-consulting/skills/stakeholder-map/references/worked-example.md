# Worked example (illustrative; fictional group consolidating finance operations from 6 business units into a shared-services centre)

| Stakeholder | Influence | Impact | Stance → target | Gains / loses | Key concern | Needs to see | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Group CFO (sponsor) | H | H | Champion → Champion | Gains cost and control | "Will BU CFOs really let go?" | BU commitment | Programme director |
| BU CFO, Retail | H | H | Sceptic → Neutral | Loses 14 FTE, direct control | "Service quality will drop and my close will slip" | SLA guarantees, opt-back clause | Group CFO |
| BU CFO, Manufacturing | H | H | Neutral → Supporter | Loses 9 FTE; gains a standardised close | "Plant accounting is too specialised" | Plant-accounting expertise retained in the centre | Group Controller |
| BU CFO, Services | M | M | Supporter → Champion | Gains scalability for growth | — | Early wave and a co-design role | Programme director |
| Finance staff in BUs (60 people) | M (collectively) | H | Anxious / sceptic → Neutral | Job changes, relocation | "Will I lose my job or have to move?" | Clear options: redeployment, transfer, support | CHRO + BU heads |
| Internal Audit Head | M | M | Neutral → Supporter | Gains standard controls | "Segregation of duties during transition" | Control design and transition testing | CRO |
| CIO | H | M | Neutral → Supporter | More ERP work, no budget | "Another unfunded mandate" | Funded ERP harmonisation budget | Group CFO |
| Employee representatives (2 plants) | M | H | Unknown → Neutral | — | Job security | Early consultation, redeployment commitments | CHRO |

**Influence paths:**
- The Retail BU CFO trusts the Group Controller (former colleague) more than the programme team.
- The CEO listens closely to the Manufacturing BU head on operational risk.

**Coalition:**
- **The Group CFO announces personally** and chairs the monthly SteerCo.
- **The Services BU CFO goes in wave 1** and presents the results at the leadership forum.
- **The Retail CFO is invited to co-design the SLA framework.**

**Engagement plan (extract):**
| Who | Objective | Message | Evidence / concession | Messenger | Channel | When | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Retail BU CFO | Neutral by wave-2 decision | "You keep control of decisions; we take the transactions, with guaranteed service" | SLA with penalties; 6-month opt-back; Retail-dedicated team lead | Group Controller, then the Group CFO | 1:1, then the SLA workshop | Weeks 2-4 | Group CFO |
| BU finance staff | Reduce anxiety; retain key talent | "Clear options for everyone, decided with you" | Options window; retention bonus for critical roles; reskilling | BU CFO + HR partner | Town halls, then 1:1s | Week 3 onward | CHRO |
| CIO | Supporter | "Funded ERP harmonisation that also fixes your data issues" | ₹6 Cr ring-fenced budget | Group CFO | 1:1 | Week 1 | Group CFO |

**Review:** stances updated after every SteerCo; the map is shared only with the core programme team.
