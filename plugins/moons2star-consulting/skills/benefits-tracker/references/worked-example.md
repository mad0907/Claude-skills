# Worked example (illustrative; procurement transformation promising ₹60 Cr over 3 years)

| # | Benefit | Type | Measure | Baseline | Target and timing | Owner | Dependencies | How banked | Conf. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Packaging price reduction | Cash-releasing | (Baseline price − new price) × volume | ₹182 Cr spend at FY26 prices (ERP, Mar 2026) | 7% = ₹12.7 Cr/yr from Q2 FY27 | CPO | Supplier consolidation (3 → 2); spec change | BU budgets cut on contract signature | High |
| 2 | Logistics rate renegotiation | Cash-releasing | Rate × lanes × volume | ₹96 Cr (FY26) | 5% = ₹4.8 Cr/yr from Q3 FY27 | Head Logistics | Tender | Logistics budget | Medium |
| 3 | Indirect spend under contract | Cash-releasing | Leakage reduction on off-contract spend | 38% off-contract (Jan 2026 audit) | 15% by Q4 FY27; ₹6 Cr/yr | CFO | Procure-to-pay workflow; catalogue | Opex budgets | Medium |
| 4 | Working capital: payment terms 45 → 60 days | Working capital (one-off cash) | Δdays × daily spend | 45 days (Mar 2026) | 60 days for 70% of ~₹1,200 Cr annual spend by Q4 FY27 = 15 days × ₹3.3 Cr/day × 70% ≈ ₹35 Cr one-off cash | Treasurer | Supplier negotiation; MSME exclusion (statutory payment limits for MSME suppliers) | Cash-flow plan | Medium |
| 5 | Procurement team productivity | Non-cash | POs per buyer per month | 210 (FY26) | 300 by FY28 | CPO | Workflow tool | Hiring avoided: 4 FTE (₹0.8 Cr) | Medium |

**Dis-benefits:** procurement-tool licences (₹1.1 Cr/yr); a temporary dip in PO cycle time during roll-out.
**Leading indicators:** % of spend on new contracts; catalogue usage; off-contract spend %; supplier acceptance of the new terms.
**Attribution:** packaging savings from product redesign by R&D are excluded from #1 (claimed by the R&D programme); Finance-agreed rule.

**Tracking (FY27, ₹ Cr):**

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | 0 | 3.2 | 2.6 | −0.6 | One supplier delayed the switch by 6 weeks; recovered in Q3 |
| 2 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | Tender closes in Q2 |
| 3 | 0.5 | 0.3 | 1.0 | 1.1 | −0.1 | Catalogue adoption 62% (target 70%) |

**Governance:** monthly owner review; quarterly SteerCo; Finance sign-off on banked amounts.
**Post-implementation review:** Q2 FY29, by internal audit.
