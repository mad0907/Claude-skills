# Sources (public standards and guidance; verify legal specifics)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Benefits management:** public-sector programme guidance on benefits maps, registers, profiles and realisation reviews; ISO 21503 (programme management) on benefits.
- **Investment appraisal and post-implementation review:** public appraisal guidance (e.g. UK HM Treasury *Green Book* on monitoring and evaluation).
- **India MSME payment rules:** MSMED Act, 2006 provisions on payment timelines to MSME suppliers, and the related income-tax provision on deductibility of delayed MSME payments (verify the current rules) before extending payment terms.
- **Management accounting:** budgeting and variance analysis for banking benefits.
