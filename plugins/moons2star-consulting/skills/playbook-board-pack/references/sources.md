# Sources (public laws, standards and widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **India (check the latest versions):**
  - Companies Act 2013: Section 166 (directors' duties), Section 179 (board powers), Section 180 (borrowing limits requiring shareholder approval), Section 188 (related-party transactions).
  - SEBI LODR Regulations: board and committee composition, related-party approvals, disclosure of material events (Regulation 30).
  - SEBI PIT Regulations (insider lists, trading windows).
  - Secretarial Standard SS-1 on board meetings (notice, agenda, quorum, minutes), issued by the company-secretaries' institute.
- **Governance practice:** board-paper quality principles (decision clarity, options, risk); the role of committees; pre-briefing practice.
- **Sector regulators:** for banks, insurers and NBFCs, RBI / IRDAI governance directions.
- **Verify all statutory references** with the company secretary or legal counsel.
