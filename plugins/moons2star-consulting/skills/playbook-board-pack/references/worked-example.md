# Worked example (illustrative; a listed specialty-chemicals company seeking board approval for a ₹650 Cr greenfield plant, part-funded by ₹400 Cr of debt)

**Decision sought:** approve the capex of ₹650 Cr and the borrowing of up to ₹400 Cr.

**Draft resolution:** "RESOLVED THAT the Board approves capital expenditure of up to ₹650 crore for the new plant at [site], as set out in the paper, and authorises borrowing of up to ₹400 crore on the terms outlined, subject to the borrowing limits approved by shareholders…"

**Director map:**
- Chair (former banker): leverage and covenants.
- Audit committee chair (CA): accounting, contingencies, controls.
- Independent director (industry veteran): technology and demand.
- Woman independent director (ESG background): environmental clearances and community issues.
- Promoter directors: growth and timing.

**Stage 3 annexes:** demand study; business case (IRR 17.8%, payback ~6 years); sensitivity (break-even utilisation 58%); risk register (clearances, technology scale-up, price cycles).

**Governance check:**
- Capex is above the CEO's delegated limit, so board approval is needed. ✓
- Borrowing is within the shareholder-approved limit under the Companies Act. ✓
- No related-party element. Technology licensing is from an unrelated party. ✓
- Disclosure: the board decision is price-sensitive, so stock-exchange disclosure is needed within the prescribed timeline after the meeting; the insider list has been updated. ✓
- Environmental clearance: applied for, awaited. Approval is made subject to it. ✓
- The audit committee reviewed the funding plan. ✓

**Director Q&A annex (excerpt):**
- *"What if prices fall back to the FY24 trough?"* IRR falls to 11%, still above the cost of capital of 10.5%, but with thin headroom. The phasing option is described in section 6.
- *"Net debt to EBITDA peak?"* 2.4x in FY28, against a covenant of 3.0x.
- *"What if the environmental clearance is delayed?"* Each 6-month delay cuts IRR by ~1.2 points. No major orders are placed before clearance.

**Red-team fixes made:** added a phased option (Phase 1 of ₹420 Cr); disclosed the customer-concentration risk (2 customers take 40% of planned volume).

**Pre-briefs:** the audit committee chair (funding and controls); the Chair (leverage).

**Presentation:** 8 minutes; 4 slides (the ask, the case, risks and mitigations, phasing).

**Points to minute:** the conditions (clearance), the directors' questions on leverage and the answers given, and the phasing option considered.
