# Sources (public standards and widely taught practices; verify current versions)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (2025)** and OWASP guidance on agentic AI threats: prompt injection (including indirect injection via documents), excessive agency, insecure output handling, sensitive information disclosure.
- **NIST AI RMF 1.0** and **NIST AI 600-1 GenAI Profile:** governance, human oversight, monitoring.
- **ISO/IEC 42001:2023:** AI management system controls; **ISO/IEC 27001:2022:** information security, including access control and logging.
- **Internal control frameworks:** the COSO Internal Control - Integrated Framework (2013): segregation of duties, authorisation limits, audit trails. For India, Companies Act 2013 s.134(5)(e) internal financial controls.
- **Levels of automation:** widely used human-automation interaction scales (from "human does all" to "fully autonomous") adapted here as L0-L4.
- **Engineering practice for agents:** prefer simple, composable workflows; give agents well-documented tools; add human checkpoints; test extensively in sandboxes. This is consistent with current published guidance from model developers and practitioners.
- **EU AI Act** Art. 14 human oversight for high-risk systems (where in scope; verify dates as amended in 2026).
