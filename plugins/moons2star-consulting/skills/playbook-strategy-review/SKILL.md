# Playbook: Strategy Review

*moons2star Consultant's Toolkit · created by Dr. Munmun Das · [moons2star.in](https://moons2star.in)*

**What this gives you:** a strategy that makes real choices, where to play and how to win, backed by facts and numbers, stress-tested, and translated into a plan people can execute, delivered stage by stage so leaders can steer along the way.

## The senior standard
1. **A strategy is a set of choices,** including what not to do. A list of aspirations isn't a strategy.
2. **Fact base before opinions.** Market, competition, customers and own economics, from validated sources.
3. **Genuine options.** At least three distinct, feasible options, including a credible status quo, evaluated on agreed criteria.
4. **Robust under uncertainty.** Test the recommendation across scenarios; define signposts and triggers.
5. **Numbers make it real:** financial impact, investment, capability needs and risks.
6. **Execution is part of strategy:** roadmap, operating model, KPIs and governance.
7. **Stage gates.** Leadership reviews and steers at each stage; no "big reveal" at the end.

## Before you start: diagnose
- The trigger (performance, disruption, new leader, cycle); the scope (group, business unit); the horizon.
- The decision-makers and governance (CEO, board, strategy committee).
- Constraints: capital, regulation, ownership, non-negotiables.
- Timeline and team; existing strategy documents and data.

## Method (stages, skills and gates)
| Stage | Purpose | Skills | Output | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Frame | Define the question, scope, criteria | `problem-statement`, `stakeholder-map`, `issue-tree`, `engagement-workplan` | Strategic question, success criteria, workplan | Sponsor agrees the question and criteria |
| 2. Fact base | Shared reality | `research-plan`, `market-sizing`, `customer-segmentation`, `competitor-benchmark`, `five-forces`, `pestel-scan`, `source-validation` | Validated fact base | Leadership accepts the fact base |
| 3. Diagnose | Position and capabilities | `value-chain`, `vrio-analysis`, `swot-tows`, `portfolio-matrix`, `kpi-driver-tree` | Diagnosis: where we win and lose, and why | Agreement on the diagnosis |
| 4. Options | Generate and evaluate | `growth-options`, `strategic-options`, `scenario-planning`, `build-buy-partner` | 3-5 options scored on criteria and across scenarios | Shortlist of 2-3 |
| 5. Choose and test | Recommendation | `business-case`, `sensitivity-analysis`, `red-team-review`, `pre-mortem` | Recommended strategy with numbers, risks and signposts | Leadership decision |
| 6. Story | Communicate | `insight-synthesis`, `scqa-storyline`, `ghost-deck`, `action-titles`, `executive-summary`, `deck-quality-check` | Leadership or board deck | Board approval (via `playbook-board-pack`) |
| 7. Execute | Translate to action | `implementation-roadmap`, `operating-model`, `raci-builder`, `change-plan`, `kpi-dashboard`, `benefits-tracker` | Roadmap, operating model, KPIs, governance | Programme launched |

**For each stage:** state the inputs needed, produce the output, summarise the decisions needed at the gate, then proceed.

## Judgement calls
- **Time-pressed:** a compressed version of the fact base (the top 10 facts), 3 options, and a 1-day leadership workshop (`workshop-design`).
- **Turnaround context:** prioritise cash, the core and cost (`cost-takeout`) before growth options.
- **Disruption context:** heavier scenario work; option value; staged commitments.
- **Group strategy:** portfolio roles and capital allocation; parenting advantage.
- **Involve the people who will execute** in stages 3-5, or adoption suffers.

## Red flags
- A strategy that is a list of initiatives.
- No fact base, or one that is unvalidated.
- Options that are variants of one idea.
- No numbers; no scenarios.
- No owners, KPIs or roadmap.
- A final big reveal with no stage gates.

## Variants by context
- **Business-unit strategy:** within group constraints; synergies with other units.
- **Functional strategy (IT, HR, operations):** link to business strategy; service levels; cost.
- **Public-sector or not-for-profit:** mission impact as the criterion; stakeholders; funding.
- **Family business:** family governance, succession, risk appetite.
- **Digital or AI strategy:** combine with `playbook-ai-roadmap`.

## House rules
- Never invent facts, figures, quotes or sources. Label estimates "(est.)" and list assumptions openly.
- If key inputs are missing, ask up to 3 short questions, or proceed with clearly stated assumptions if the user wants speed.
- Answer first, then support. Plain English; define any jargon.
- Use the user's currency, units, terminology and context (e.g. ₹ and lakh/crore for Indian users).
- Do calculations with code or a spreadsheet when available, not mental arithmetic.
- For legal, regulatory, tax or accounting specifics, flag them for verification against current official sources and qualified advisers.
- Remind users not to paste confidential or personal data they are not permitted to share; suggest anonymising.
