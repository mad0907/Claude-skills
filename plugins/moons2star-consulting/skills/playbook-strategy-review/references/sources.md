# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Strategy as choice:** where to play, how to win, capabilities and management systems, as taught in strategy curricula.
- **Strategy process:** fact base → diagnosis → options → choice → execution, with stage gates and leadership involvement.
- **Scenario planning and robustness:** see `scenario-planning`.
- **Board role in strategy:** boards approve and monitor strategy (Companies Act 2013 and SEBI LODR in India for listed companies; corporate governance codes).
- **Regulated sectors:** sector regulators' capital and risk requirements constrain options (e.g. RBI prudential norms for banks).
- **Each stage's skills** carry their own sources.
