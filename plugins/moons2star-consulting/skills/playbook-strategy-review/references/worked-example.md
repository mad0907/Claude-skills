# Worked example (illustrative; a regional private bank, ₹85,000 Cr balance sheet, 3-year strategy refresh in 10 weeks)

**Strategic question:** how do we lift return on equity from 11% to 15% by FY29, while staying within our risk appetite and capital limits?

**Success criteria:** ROE ≥15%; CET1 ≥13%; gross NPA ≤3%; customer satisfaction up.

**Stage 2 fact base (top facts, validated):**
- Our cost-to-income is 58% vs a peer median of 49%.
- Retail deposits grew 9% vs 13% for peers.
- MSME lending in our home states is growing ~16% a year; our share is 4%.
- Digital channels handle 38% of transactions vs 70%+ for leading peers.
- Gold-loan yields are attractive, but competition is intensifying.

**Stage 3 diagnosis:**
- We win in relationship-led MSME and affluent banking in 3 home states (VRIO: local relationships and credit judgement are valuable, rare and hard to imitate).
- We lose on cost and digital deposits.
- The unsecured retail book is sub-scale.

**Stage 4 options:**

| Option | Description | ROE FY29 (est.) | Capital need | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Status quo plus | Incremental efficiency | 12.0% | Low | Low |
| B. MSME champion | Focus on MSME in home states plus adjacent states; a digital MSME platform; supply-chain finance | 15.3% | Medium | Medium (credit) |
| C. National digital retail | Scale unsecured digital lending nationally | 14.1% | High | High (credit, competition) |
| D. B + cost programme | B, plus a 7-point reduction in cost-to-income through branch rationalisation and automation | 16.0% | Medium | Medium |

**Scenarios:** a rate-cut cycle, an MSME credit stress, and a digital-competition shock. D stays ≥14% in 2 of 3 scenarios. C falls to 10% in the credit-stress scenario.

**Stage 5 recommendation:** Option D, MSME champion plus cost.
- **Red-team:** the credit-quality assumptions for adjacent states are the weakest link.
- **The fix:** a phased entry with strict underwriting gates.
- **Signposts:** MSME stress indicators; deposit growth.

**Strategy on a page:**
- **Where to play:** MSME and affluent customers in 5 states.
- **Where not to play:** national unsecured digital lending.
- **How to win:** relationship plus a digital platform; supply-chain finance with anchor corporates.
- **Must-win battles:** an MSME platform live in 12 months; deposit growth through transaction banking; cost-to-income ≤51% by FY29; credit quality.
- **Roadmap and KPIs:** via `implementation-roadmap` and `kpi-dashboard`.
