# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Evidence synthesis:** narrative and systematic review methods (question framing, extraction, quality appraisal, synthesis), as taught in research-methods curricula and evidence-based-management practice.
- **Grading evidence strength:** approaches that weigh study quality, consistency and independence (adapted from evidence-grading systems used in health and policy research).
- **Triangulation:** combining multiple sources and methods to corroborate findings.
- **Source validation:** see the `source-validation` skill.
