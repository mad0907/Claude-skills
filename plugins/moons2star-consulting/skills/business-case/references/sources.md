# Sources (public guidance and widely taught methods; verify current versions)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **UK HM Treasury, *The Green Book*** (central government guidance on appraisal and evaluation): options appraisal, do-minimum baseline, optimism-bias adjustments, the five-case model (strategic, economic, commercial, financial, management). Freely available and widely adapted by private-sector firms.
- **Reference-class forecasting and optimism bias:** widely documented evidence that large projects tend to overrun on cost and under-deliver benefits. Use your organisation's own history of past projects as the reference class where possible.
- **Capital budgeting:** NPV, IRR, payback, incremental cash flows, sunk and opportunity costs, as taught in standard corporate-finance curricula.
- **Benefits realisation:** public-sector benefits-management guidance and professional project-management bodies' standards.
- **Accounting treatment:** capitalisation under IFRS / Ind AS (e.g. Ind AS 16 property, plant and equipment; Ind AS 38 intangibles, for software development). Confirm with Finance and auditors.
