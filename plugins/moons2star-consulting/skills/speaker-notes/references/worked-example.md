# Worked example (illustrative; investment committee, 20 minutes, 12 slides, pre-read sent)

**Opening (45s):** "Thank you. We're asking you to approve ₹420 crore for Phase 1 of the Dahej plant today, released in three tranches. The reason for deciding now is simple: two anchor customers qualify suppliers this year, and missing that window puts around ₹200 crore of annual revenue at risk. You've had the pre-read, so I'll take 5 minutes on the key points and then open it up."

| Slide | Time | Say | Points | Cue | Transition | Skip? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 Exec summary | 2 min | "Here's the case in one page." | NPV ₹212 Cr; IRR 18% vs 15% hurdle; break-even at −22% volume | Pause after the break-even number | "Let me show you why demand is real." | No |
| 4 Demand | 1.5 min | "The demand isn't a forecast; it's letters of intent." | 11 KTPA under LOI; qualification timeline | — | "Of course, the question is risk." | No |
| 7 Sensitivity | 1 min | "Volume is the one assumption that matters." | Break-even −22%; take-or-pay gate | Emphasise "gate" | "That's why we've staged the money." | No |
| 9 Stage gates | 1 min | "You only commit ₹160 crore today." | Tranche criteria; kill criterion | — | "Which brings me to the ask." | No |
| 5, 6, 8 | — | (backup) | Market, options, capex detail | — | — | Yes |
| 12 Ask | 30s | "We're asking for three things today." | Resolution; tranche release by the board; delegation limits | Stop and wait | — | No |

**Hard questions:**
| Question | Answer (first line) | Fact | Backup |
|---|---|---|---|
| "What if the anchors don't sign take-or-pay?" | "Then we don't release tranche 2. The maximum loss is the ₹160 Cr already committed, mostly recoverable land and long-lead items." | Recoverability est. 55% | 9 |
| "Why not debottleneck Ankleshwar instead?" | "We will, but it adds only 6 KTPA. The anchors need 11." | Options table | 6 |
| "Who's accountable for delivery?" | "Our new project director, reporting monthly to the audit committee on cost." | Appointment confirmed | 10 |
| "What does a 9-month delay cost?" | "About ₹44 Cr of NPV; still positive." | Sensitivity | 7 |

**Difficult moment:** if a director challenges the demand forecast as optimistic, say: "That's the right challenge. That's exactly why tranche 2 is conditional on signed take-or-pay contracts. If they're not there, we stop."

**Close (30s):** "So: approval of ₹420 crore in three tranches, with ₹160 crore today and tranche 2 back to this board. If you're comfortable, we'll proceed with the EPC contract this month."

**Key numbers:** ₹420 Cr / ₹160 Cr; NPV ₹212 Cr; IRR 18.4%; break-even −22% volume; 11 KTPA under LOI; net debt/EBITDA peak 1.9x.
