# Worked example (illustrative; a general insurer, ₹14,000 Cr gross premium; 18-month AI roadmap)

**Goals and value pools:**
- Reduce the claims expense ratio by 1.5 points.
- Cut fraud leakage (est. 6-8% of motor and health claims).
- Improve retail renewal from 71% to 76%.
- Lift underwriting productivity.

**Long list:** 42 use cases across claims, underwriting, distribution, service, finance and HR.

**Portfolio (top 8 of 12 prioritised):**

| Use case | Value (₹ Cr/yr, est.) | Feasibility | Risk tier | Data readiness | Wave |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Motor-claim photo triage and estimate assist | 45-60 | High | Medium | Good | 1 |
| Health-claim fraud scoring (assist investigators) | 60-90 | Medium | High (affects customers) | Medium | 1 (with human review) |
| Service-agent copilot (policy Q&A, summaries) | 20-25 | High | Low-medium | Good | 1 |
| Renewal-propensity and next-best-action | 30-40 | Medium | Medium | Medium | 2 |
| Commercial-underwriting document extraction | 15-20 | High | Low | Good | 2 |
| Health pre-authorisation agent (drafts decisions, with human approval) | 25-35 | Medium | High | Medium | 2-3 |
| Regulatory-return preparation assistant | 5-8 | High | Low | Good | 2 |
| Pricing optimisation | 50-80 | Low-medium | High (fairness, regulator) | Low | 3 (after data work) |

**Wave-1 pilots:**
- **Motor triage:** 3 regions; baseline cycle time of 9 days; target ≤6 days; estimate accuracy within ±10% for 80% of cases; scale if met for 8 weeks.
- **Fraud scoring:** an investigator-assist tool only; no automated rejection. Metrics: hit rate, false positives, fairness checks by region and age band.
- **Service copilot:** 200 agents; handle time −20%; quality audit scores no worse than baseline.

**Governance:**
- An AI policy approved by the board.
- An inventory of all AI systems.
- Risk tiering: high-risk uses need fairness testing, explainability, human review, a legal and compliance sign-off, and monitoring.
- Alignment with the DPDP Act and the regulator's guidance on outsourcing and data.
- A risk register (`ai-risk-register`) reviewed quarterly by the risk committee.

**Operating model:**
- A central AI centre of excellence: platform, standards, model risk.
- Business-embedded product owners.
- A model-risk function under the CRO.

**Change:**
- Training for 1,500 claims and service staff.
- Revised investigator workflows.
- Incentives updated (quality plus productivity).
- Union and staff communication on role changes.

| Quarter | Initiatives | Milestones | KPIs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Governance, data foundations, 3 pilots | Policy approved; pilots live | Cycle time, handle time |
| Q2 | Pilot results, scale decisions | 2 scale decisions | Claims-ratio impact |
| Q3-Q4 | Scale wave 1; start wave 2 | Motor triage national | Fraud savings, renewal |
| Q5-Q6 | Wave 2; pricing data work | Pre-auth agent pilot | Expense ratio −1.0 pt |

**Investment:** ₹85 Cr over 18 months. Expected run-rate benefits of ₹150-210 Cr a year by month 24 (validated quarterly by Finance).

**Board asks:**
- Approve the AI policy and the governance structure.
- Approve the investment, with wave gates.
