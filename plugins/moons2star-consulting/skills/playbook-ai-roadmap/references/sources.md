# Sources (public laws, standards and widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **AI management and risk standards:** ISO/IEC 42001 (AI management systems); ISO/IEC 23894 (AI risk management); NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0) and its generative-AI profile.
- **Principles:** OECD AI Principles.
- **Regulation (check current status):**
  - EU AI Act (risk-based obligations; phased application).
  - India: DPDP Act 2023 and the DPDP Rules; national AI governance guidelines; RBI's FREE-AI framework for the financial sector.
  - Sector regulators' guidance on outsourcing, model risk and data.
- **Application security for AI and LLM systems:** OWASP guidance on LLM application risks.
- **Each stage's skills** (`ai-opportunity-scan`, `ai-governance-review`, etc.) list detailed sources.
- **Verify** regulatory interpretations with legal and compliance.
