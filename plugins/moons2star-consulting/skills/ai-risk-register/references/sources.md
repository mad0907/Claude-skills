# Sources (as researched November 2026; verify before relying)

- **NIST AI RMF 1.0** (Jan 2023): especially MAP (context, impact) and MEASURE (metrics); **NIST AI 600-1 GenAI Profile** (Jul 2024): GenAI-specific risks such as confabulation, information security, data privacy and IP.
- **ISO/IEC 23894:2023** (AI risk management); **ISO/IEC 42001:2023** (AI management system, Annex A controls); **ISO/IEC 42005:2025** (AI impact assessment); **ISO 31000:2018**.
- **EU AI Act** (Reg. 2024/1689): Art. 5 prohibited practices; Art. 6 and Annex III high-risk areas (e.g. biometrics, critical infrastructure, education, employment, access to essential private and public services including creditworthiness and insurance pricing for life and health, law enforcement, migration, justice). Digital Omnibus (Reg. 2026/1744) moved Annex III obligations to 2 Dec 2027 and Annex I to 2 Aug 2028 (verify).
- **India DPDP Act 2023 / DPDP Rules 2025:** most duties from 13 May 2027; SDF duty on algorithmic software (Rule 13); log retention rules (verify).
- **RBI FREE-AI report** (Aug 2025): AI inventory, incident reporting, audit and board policy recommendations for regulated entities; **MeitY India AI Governance Guidelines** (Nov 2025).
- **OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025** (prompt injection, sensitive information disclosure, supply chain, excessive agency, misinformation and others).
- **Four-fifths (80%) rule:** originates in US EEOC Uniform Guidelines (1978) as a screening heuristic for adverse impact. Use it as an indicator, not as a legal standard in other jurisdictions.
- Sector rules to check: medical-device regulation for clinical AI (e.g. CDSCO in India); RBI digital lending directions; IRDAI guidelines; SEBI circulars on AI/ML use by intermediaries.
