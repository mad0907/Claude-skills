# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Qualitative interviewing:** semi-structured interview design, funnel questioning, neutral probing, as taught in social-science and market-research methods curricula.
- **Behaviour over opinion:** customer-discovery practice of asking about specific past events rather than hypothetical intentions (stated vs revealed preference).
- **Sample size for qualitative work:** saturation concepts (themes typically stabilise after a modest number of interviews per homogeneous segment).
- **Ethics:** informed consent, confidentiality and anonymisation; market-research codes of conduct; data protection (DPDP Act 2023 in India).
- **Expert-call compliance:** avoiding confidential and material non-public information; insider-trading rules (e.g. SEBI PIT Regulations).
