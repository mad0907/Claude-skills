# Worked example (illustrative; B2B payroll software, understanding why mid-sized customers churn, 45-minute calls)

**Decision:** whether to invest in a dedicated onboarding team vs product fixes to cut churn.

**Objectives:**
1. Understand the moments and reasons customers decide to leave.
2. Size the cost of the pain (time, errors, money).
3. Learn what they switched to and why.
4. Test whether early onboarding experience predicts churn.

**Sample (16 interviews):**
- 6 churned in the last 6 months.
- 4 at-risk (low usage).
- 4 healthy long-term customers.
- 2 lost deals (chose a competitor).
- Respondents: payroll or HR heads; include 3 finance heads (budget owners).

**Screener:** responsible for payroll decisions; involved in the selection or the exit; tenure ≥6 months in role; no current employment with a competitor.

| Time | Section | Questions | Objective |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | Opening | Purpose: "improving payroll software for companies like yours"; consent to record; confidentiality; no right answers | — |
| 4-9 | Context | Your role; payroll size and complexity (headcount, states, pay cycles); who else is involved | — |
| 9-20 | Onboarding | "Walk me through your first 60 days with us." What went well or badly? How long until your first correct payroll? How many issues needed support? | 4 |
| 20-32 | The exit (churned) or the doubt (at-risk) | "When did you first think about leaving? What happened that week?" Then: who decided, what alternatives, what it cost to switch | 1, 3 |
| 32-38 | Cost of pain | "Roughly how many hours a month on fixes or workarounds? Any penalties or employee complaints? Approximate cost?" | 2 |
| 38-42 | Hypothesis | "If you'd had a named onboarding specialist for 60 days, would that have changed anything? What specifically?" (neutral; also ask what *wouldn't* have helped) | 4 |
| 42-45 | Close | "What should I have asked?"; anyone else to speak to; thanks | — |

**Drop if short of time:** the context detail on pay cycles; the referral question.

**Note template:** ID | segment | first-correct-payroll days | support tickets in the first 60 days | trigger event | alternatives | switching cost | hours per month on fixes | key verbatim quotes | evidence for / against the onboarding hypothesis.

**Compliance:**
- Respondent data handled under data-protection obligations.
- Quotes anonymised in reporting.
- Incentive offered within ethical norms.
