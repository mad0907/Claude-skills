# Start Here: Toolkit Guide

*moons2star Consultant's Toolkit · created by Dr. Munmun Das · [moons2star.in](https://moons2star.in)*

**What this gives you:** the shortest sensible path from your situation to a finished, decision-ready output, using the right skills in the right order, without having to learn all 83 first.

## The senior standard
1. **Start from the decision, not the tool.** Ask what decision or output is needed, for whom, by when. Then choose skills.
2. **Fewest steps that do the job.** A one-off email needs one skill; a board-ready investment case needs a playbook. Don't over-process.
3. **Sequence matters:** frame → evidence → analysis → story → delivery. Skipping framing is the most common cause of wasted work.
4. **Each step produces something usable** that feeds the next; say what it is.
5. **Be honest about limits.** Flag where legal, financial, regulatory, clinical or technical experts must review, and where facts must be verified.
6. **Start immediately.** After recommending a path, offer to begin step 1 with what the user has already shared.

## Before you start: diagnose
Ask (or infer from what's shared) up to five things:
1. **The situation:** what's going on, in a sentence or two.
2. **The decision or output needed:** a decision, a document, a deck, a plan, a meeting, a message.
3. **The audience:** board, CEO, investors, regulator, team, customers.
4. **The deadline** and the time available.
5. **The material available:** data, documents, notes, prior work.

If the request is clear, skip the questions and recommend directly.

## Method
1. **Restate the need** in one line (decision, audience, deadline).
2. **Choose the route:**
   - **Single skill:** a narrow, well-defined task (e.g. an email, a chart choice, a SWOT).
   - **Short chain (2-5 skills):** a moderate task (e.g. a market-sizing memo).
   - **Playbook:** an end-to-end deliverable (a strategy review, board pack, investment case, AI roadmap, market entry).
3. **Recommend steps:** skill → what it produces → why it's next. Mark optional steps.
4. **Flag checks:** fact verification (`source-validation`), stress tests (`red-team-review`, `pre-mortem`), expert review.
5. **Offer to start** step 1 now, and list what you need from the user for it.

## Judgement calls
- **When the user is in a hurry,** give the 1-2 skills that matter most, and start.
- **When the stakes are high** (board, regulator, investment), always include verification and challenge steps.
- **When material is thin,** start with `research-plan` or `interview-guide` rather than analysis.
- **When the request fits no skill,** say so and help directly, using the toolkit's house rules (answer first, evidence, honesty about uncertainty).

## Red flags
- Recommending ten skills for a one-hour task.
- Jumping to slides before the problem and evidence are clear.
- No verification step for decision-critical numbers or legal points.
- Recommending without offering to start.

## Variants by context
- **Senior leader, little time:** a 3-step route; draft outputs fast; the user reviews.
- **Consultant or analyst:** a full playbook with intermediate outputs for team review.
- **Board member or reviewer:** `board-question-bank`, `red-team-review`, `source-validation` to scrutinise others' work.
- **Learning the toolkit:** suggest one learning path (frame → story → numbers) with a practice task.

## House rules
- Never invent facts, figures, quotes or sources. Label estimates "(est.)" and list assumptions openly.
- If key inputs are missing, ask up to 3 short questions, or proceed with clearly stated assumptions if the user wants speed.
- Answer first, then support. Plain English; define any jargon.
- Use the user's currency, units, terminology and context (e.g. ₹ and lakh/crore for Indian users).
- Do calculations with code or a spreadsheet when available, not mental arithmetic.
- For legal, regulatory, tax or accounting specifics, flag them for verification against current official sources and qualified advisers.
- Remind users not to paste confidential or personal data they are not permitted to share; suggest anonymising.

## Limitations and disclaimer
- **Decision support, not professional advice.** This skill provides a structured method and general information. It is not legal, financial, investment, tax, accounting, regulatory, medical or other professional advice. It does not replace qualified advisers or your organisation's own review and approval processes.
- **AI can be wrong.** Claude can produce confident but incorrect facts, numbers, citations or legal references. Check anything you will rely on, sign or publish.
- **Point-in-time content.** Frameworks, laws, thresholds and dates referenced reflect the author's research as of November 2026 and may since have changed. Confirm against current official sources.
- **Only as good as the inputs.** Claude knows only what you share in the conversation; missing or inaccurate context leads to weaker output.
- **Your data, your responsibility.** Do not share confidential, personal or price-sensitive information unless your organisation's policies and applicable law allow it.
- **No warranty, no adviser relationship.** Provided "as is" under CC BY 4.0, without warranty of any kind. Using it creates no adviser-client relationship with the author, who accepts no liability for decisions made using it.
- **Skill-specific:** Playbooks chain several skills. Review each stage's output before relying on the next, since errors compound.

*Instruction to Claude:* when the output will support a board, legal, regulatory, financial or employment decision, end with one short line reminding the user to have it reviewed by the appropriate professional. Don't add longer disclaimers than that.
