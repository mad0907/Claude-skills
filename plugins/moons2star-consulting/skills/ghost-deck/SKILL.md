# Ghost Deck

*moons2star Consultant's Toolkit · created by Dr. Munmun Das · [moons2star.in](https://moons2star.in)*

**What this gives you:** the whole deck on one page before anyone builds a slide. Analysis is focused on what the story needs, data gaps are visible early, and the team knows exactly which exhibit each person is producing.

## The senior standard
1. **Hypothesis titles.** Write titles as you expect the answer to be. Mark them as hypotheses and change them as evidence arrives. It's a plan, not a conclusion.
2. **Exhibit sketch per slide.** Describe the chart that would prove the title (type, axes, comparison, highlight) and the data it needs. This turns the storyline into a work plan.
3. **Story discipline.** Sections follow the storyline; each slide earns its place; the main deck fits the length limit (e.g. 10-15 slides for an ExCo).
4. **Visible gaps.** Status per slide (data in hand / data requested / hypothesis only) shows risk to the timeline.
5. **Iterate.** Update the ghost deck weekly; it becomes the final deck.

## Before you start: diagnose
- The key question, storyline or hypothesis tree.
- Audience, length limit and format.
- The team and timeline (for owners and dates).

## Method
1. **Sections from the storyline:**
   - executive summary;
   - situation and complication;
   - findings (one section per key-line point);
   - options and recommendation;
   - plan, risks and the ask;
   - appendix.
2. **Slide list:** for each, a draft action title (hypothesis); flag uncertain ones.
3. **Exhibit sketch:**
   - chart type;
   - x and y axes or table columns;
   - comparison (vs plan, peers, time, segments);
   - what to highlight;
   - source.
4. **Data needed and status;** link to the data request list.
5. **Owner and due date** per slide.
6. **Main deck vs appendix:** keep the main deck within the limit; move supporting detail to the appendix.
7. **Read the titles only:** does the story hold? Fix gaps.
8. **Review with the sponsor** early. Agreeing the ghost deck aligns expectations before analysis effort is spent.

## Judgement calls
- **If a hypothesis title turns out wrong, celebrate.** Change the title and the story; that's the method working.
- **Keep alternative storylines in mind** when evidence is mixed. Sketch the slide you'd show if the opposite were true.
- **Paper or whiteboard first** for team sessions. Fast iteration beats polished software early.

## Red flags
- Slides with topic titles and no exhibit sketch.
- A main deck of 40 slides.
- No status or owner; data needs discovered late.
- The ghost deck never updated after week 1.

## Variants by context
- **Board decks:** shorter (8-12 slides) with a paper; the ghost deck aligns with the paper's sections.
- **Due diligence reports:** sections by diligence area; red-flag slides first.
- **Investor pitches:** fixed narrative arc (problem, solution, market, traction, model, team, ask).
- **Workshops:** the ghost deck includes the exercise slides and the expected outputs.

## Output format

```markdown
## Ghost Deck: [title], [audience], max [n] slides

| # | Section | Draft action title (hypothesis) | Exhibit sketch (type; axes; comparison; highlight) | Data needed | Status | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Appendix slides:** ...
**Titles-only read:** ...
**Biggest data risks:** ...
```

## House rules
- Never invent facts, figures, quotes or sources. Label estimates "(est.)" and list assumptions openly.
- If key inputs are missing, ask up to 3 short questions, or proceed with clearly stated assumptions if the user wants speed.
- Answer first, then support. Plain English; define any jargon.
- Use the user's currency, units, terminology and context (e.g. ₹ and lakh/crore for Indian users).
- Do calculations with code or a spreadsheet when available, not mental arithmetic.
- For legal, regulatory, tax or accounting specifics, flag them for verification against current official sources and qualified advisers.
- Remind users not to paste confidential or personal data they are not permitted to share; suggest anonymising.
