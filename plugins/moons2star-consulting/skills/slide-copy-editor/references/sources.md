# Sources (public standards and widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **ISO 24495-1:2023:** plain language (principles: relevant, findable, understandable, usable).
- **Public-sector plain-language and style guides:** concise sentences, active voice, defined terms, consistent number formats.
- **Advertising standards:** substantiation of claims (national advertising self-regulatory codes and consumer-protection law for consumer-facing claims).
- **Editing practice:** parallelism, concision and consistency, as taught in professional editing and business-writing courses.
