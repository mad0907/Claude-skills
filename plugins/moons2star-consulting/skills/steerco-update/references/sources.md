# Sources (public standards and widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Programme and project governance:** ISO 21502:2020 (project management) and ISO 21503 (programme management): reporting, tolerances, escalation.
- **Management by exception:** tolerance-based escalation, a widely used project-governance principle.
- **Earned value and forecasting basics:** forecast at completion, variance analysis (project-controls practice).
- **Benefits management:** public-sector programme guidance on tracking benefits during delivery.
