# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Resource-based view of the firm and the VRIO framework:** core strategy-curriculum concepts on how valuable, rare, inimitable and organised resources create sustained competitive advantage.
- **Isolating mechanisms:** historical conditions, causal ambiguity, social complexity, legal protection, network effects and economies of scale.
- **Dynamic capabilities:** the ability to sense, seize and reconfigure resources as environments change; relevant to durability assessments.
- **Core competence concept:** competencies that provide access to multiple markets, contribute to customer value, and are hard to imitate.
