# Sources (public standards and widely taught practice)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Information-design principles:** maximise the data-to-ink ratio; prefer position and length encodings (bars, lines, bullet charts) over angle and area (gauges, pies); show trends and targets; use small multiples for comparisons. These are standard in data-visualisation curricula.
- **Performance management:** leading and lagging indicators; balanced-scorecard-style coverage of financial, customer, process and people perspectives; target-setting and tolerance practice.
- **Data quality and governance:** ISO 8000 (data quality); data-lineage and metric-definition (semantic layer) practices.
- **ISO 22400:** KPIs for manufacturing operations; healthcare quality indicators from national accreditation standards (e.g. NABH in India) for clinical metric definitions.
- **Board reporting:** risk-appetite metrics and governance reporting (see the `risk-appetite` and `board-paper` skills).
