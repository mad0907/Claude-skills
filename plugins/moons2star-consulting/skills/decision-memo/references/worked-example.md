# Worked example (illustrative; fictional retail bank)

# Decision: whether to pilot AI-assisted 24x7 customer support, by 30 November

**Recommendation and ask:** pilot AI-assisted 24x7 support for English-language retail banking queries for 90 days (₹1.1 Cr), with go/no-go on 15 March. We ask the ExCo to approve the pilot and the guardrails below.

## Context
- Contact volume rose 22% this year.
- 38% of calls arrive outside our 8am-8pm hours and go to voicemail or a callback.
- Complaint-response time breaches have doubled.
- Hiring a night shift (45 staff) would cost ₹6.8 Cr a year.

## Options
- **Status quo:** no cost, but out-of-hours service gaps persist. Breach rates and complaints are likely to keep rising.
- **A. Human night shift:** full coverage and the highest quality, but ₹6.8 Cr a year, and recruitment takes 6 months.
- **B. AI-assisted 24x7 (recommended for pilot):**
  - An AI assistant answers routine queries, grounded in approved content.
  - It hands over to a smaller human night team (8 staff) for complex or sensitive cases.
  - ₹1.1 Cr pilot; est. ₹2.4 Cr a year at scale.

| | Status quo | A. Night shift | B. AI-assisted 24x7 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Benefits | None | Full human coverage | 24x7 coverage of routine queries; faster responses |
| Costs | Rising complaints and penalties | ₹6.8 Cr/yr | ₹1.1 Cr pilot; ~₹2.4 Cr/yr at scale |
| Risks | Regulatory scrutiny of service | Hiring delays; attrition | Wrong answers; customer trust; data protection |
| Time / reversibility | — | 6 months; hard to reverse | 6 weeks; easy to stop |

## Recommendation and rationale
Option B meets the service gap at about a third of A's cost, can start in 6 weeks, and is easy to stop.
**Trade-off:** we accept some risk of AI errors on routine queries in exchange for speed and cost. This is mitigated by grounding, human handover and close monitoring.

## Risks, mitigations and reversal triggers
- **Wrong answers:** answers come only from approved content, with citations; refusal plus handover when unsure; 5% QA sampling.
- **Vulnerable customers:** detection of hardship and bereavement keywords triggers immediate human handover.
- **Data:** processing stays in our cloud tenancy; no vendor training on our data.
- **Reversal triggers:**
  - CSAT below 4.0 for 2 consecutive weeks;
  - any critical regulatory-language breach;
  - an escalation rate above 45%.
  Any one of these pauses the pilot.

## Implementation
- **Owner:** Head of Customer Service.
- **Weeks 1-6:** build and test (offline evaluation on 800 queries).
- **Weeks 7-19:** live pilot.
- **15 March:** go/no-go.

## Approvals
- AI risk committee (scheduled 20 Nov).
- Privacy review (in progress).
- Union consultation on the night-team roles (completed).
