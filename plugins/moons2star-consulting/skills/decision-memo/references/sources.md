# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Narrative memos in place of slides:** a widely adopted executive practice, in which a short written narrative is read at the start of a meeting to improve decision quality.
- **Decision quality principles:** frame, alternatives, information, values and trade-offs, reasoning, commitment (decision-analysis curricula).
- **Options appraisal:** public appraisal guidance (e.g. UK HM Treasury *Green Book*) on status quo / do-minimum baselines.
- **Decision records:** architecture-decision and governance-minute practices for capturing rationale and dissent.
