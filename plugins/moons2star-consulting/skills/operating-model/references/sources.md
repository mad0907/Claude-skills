# Sources (widely taught concepts; verify legal specifics)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Operating-model design:** multi-element models (structure, process, people, technology, governance, location, performance, culture), as taught in organisation-design and strategy-execution curricula.
- **Organisation design principles:** spans and layers, decision rights, differentiation and integration, centres of excellence, shared services.
- **Capability-based planning:** distinguishing differentiating vs commodity capabilities.
- **Global capability centres:** evolution models from transactional delivery to product and innovation ownership (industry body reports; verify current data).
- **Employment law** (India and other jurisdictions): consultation, transfer and redundancy rules for people changes (verify with HR and counsel).
- **AI governance roles:** NIST AI RMF GOVERN function; ISO/IEC 42001 roles and responsibilities.
