# Worked example (illustrative; target operating model for a new AI and data GCC in India serving a US parent)

**Design principles:**
1. The GCC owns data and AI products end-to-end, with US business co-owners.
2. One global data platform and one set of AI standards.
3. Business value is measured per product, not per headcount.
4. AI risk oversight is independent of delivery teams.
5. Talent is grown through clear career paths in India.

| Element | Current | Target | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Value / services | Ad hoc analytics support; tickets from 9 US teams | 6 data and AI product lines with roadmaps (pricing, customer analytics, finance automation, risk models, GenAI platform, data platform) | From services to products |
| Processes | Request-driven; no intake prioritisation | Quarterly portfolio planning; product discovery; an MLOps lifecycle | New intake and portfolio process |
| Structure | 40 people under 3 managers by skill | Product teams (product manager + engineers + data scientists) in 2 tribes; platform team; AI governance cell | Skill pools → cross-functional teams |
| Decision rights | US approves everything | GCC head decides internal model deployment; US business owner decides customer-facing release; AI risk cell can veto high-risk launches | Delegated, with guardrails |
| Locations / sourcing | 100% Bengaluru; contractors 30% | Bengaluru hub + Pune satellite; contractors ≤15%; partner for 24x7 platform operations | Reduce contractor dependency |
| Technology / data | Copies of US data in multiple places | One cloud data platform; access via governed data products | Platform build in year 1 |
| Performance | Utilisation % | Product outcome KPIs; adoption; value delivered | New scorecards |
| Culture | Order-taking | Ownership; experimentation with guardrails; knowledge-sharing guilds | Leadership role-modelling; rituals |

**Structural options:**
- **A.** Functional pools (status quo+).
- **B.** Product tribes + platform (chosen).
- **C.** Embedded teams inside each US business.

B best fits principles 1-3. C fragments standards; A keeps order-taking.

**Key decision rights:**
| Decision | Recommends | Agrees | Decides | Executes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product roadmap priority | GCC product manager | US business owner | Joint portfolio council | Product team |
| Deploy an internal ML model | Product team | AI risk cell (tier check) | GCC head | Platform team |
| Customer-facing GenAI release | Product team | AI risk cell, Legal | US business owner | Product team |

**Sizing:** 40 → 85 FTE over 18 months; cost per FTE +8% (higher skill mix); contractors −50%.
**Transition:**
- Q1: product lines defined; first 2 tribes.
- Q2-Q3: platform build; hiring.
- Q4: decision rights live.
- Throughout: monthly communications to US stakeholders.
