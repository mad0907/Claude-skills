# Worked example (illustrative; an Indian B2B HR and payroll software company, ₹220 Cr revenue, seeking its first international market)

**Objective:** a profitable international revenue stream reaching ₹60 Cr ARR within 5 years. It must reuse the product core and be within a ₹30 Cr peak investment.

**Criteria:**
- Attractiveness (40%): market size, growth, willingness to pay.
- Ability to win (40%): product fit with local payroll rules, Indian-diaspora business networks, competitor strength.
- Regulatory feasibility (20%): data residency, ease of doing business, payroll-law complexity.

**Screen (scores 1-5):**

| Market | Attractiveness | Ability to win | Regulatory feasibility | Weighted | Rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| UAE | 3.5 | 4.2 | 4.5 | **3.98** | 1 |
| Saudi Arabia | 4.5 | 3.4 | 3.2 | **3.80** | 2 |
| Indonesia | 4.2 | 2.6 | 3.0 | 3.32 | 3 |
| Vietnam | 3.4 | 2.4 | 3.2 | 2.96 | 4= |
| Kenya | 2.6 | 3.0 | 3.6 | 2.96 | 4= |

**Deep-dive (UAE first, Saudi Arabia as wave 2):**
- **Market:** ~9,000 companies with 50-1,000 employees in the target segments (est., from licensing-authority and chamber data; to be validated).
- **Regulation:** wage-protection-system compliance is a must-have; local data-hosting expectations in some sectors.
- **Customers:** 14 interviews. Pain points are end-of-service gratuity calculations, multi-nationality payroll and visa-linked HR documents.
- **Competitors:** 3 regional players are strong in enterprise; the mid-market is underserved; global suites are expensive.

**Proposition and pricing:**
- Mid-market payroll plus HR with built-in local compliance and Arabic and English interfaces.
- Priced 20-25% below global suites.
- Per-employee-per-month tiers.

**Entry mode:**
- An organic subsidiary with a local reseller partnership for SME reach.
- Acquisition was considered but rejected: targets are overpriced, and integration would absorb scarce management time.
- A JV is not needed for a software business in the target free zones (verify the licensing route).

**Business case (₹ Cr, 7 years; hurdle 14%):**

| Year | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cash flow | −18 | −9 | 2 | 8 | 14 | 18 | 22 | 24 |
| Cumulative | −18 | −27 | −25 | −17 | −3 | 15 | 37 | 61 |

- **Base case:** NPV ₹18.3 Cr; IRR ~28%; payback ~4.2 years; peak funding ₹27 Cr (within the ₹30 Cr limit).
- **Downside (inflows −30%):** NPV ₹5.0 Cr; IRR ~18%.
- **Switching value:** inflows from year 2 can fall ~41% before NPV turns negative.

**Staging and gates:**
- **Gate 1 (month 12):** ≥25 paying customers, gross retention ≥90%, and compliance certification done. If met, add the sales team and the reseller network.
- **Gate 2 (month 24):** ₹12 Cr ARR. If met, prepare Saudi Arabia entry.

**Risks:**
- Compliance errors in payroll (very high impact): local payroll experts plus pre-run checks.
- Sales-cycle length.
- Partner dependence.
- Currency risk.

**Exit criteria:** fewer than 15 customers at month 12, or a CAC payback of more than 30 months at month 18. Either triggers a stop-or-pivot review.

**Launch plan:** a free-zone entity, a local payroll-compliance lead, 2 reseller partners, and 5 design-partner customers in the first 6 months.

**KPIs:** ARR, logos, net revenue retention, CAC payback, compliance incidents.

**Decision requested:** approve Phase 1 (₹18 Cr) and the gated release of up to ₹9 Cr more.
