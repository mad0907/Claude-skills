# Sources (public data, laws and widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Market selection:** screening on attractiveness and ability to win; weighted scoring; psychic and institutional distance, as taught in international-business curricula.
- **Entry modes:** export, licensing or franchising, partnership, joint venture, acquisition, greenfield; staged internationalisation.
- **Indian outbound investment rules:** the Overseas Investment Rules and Regulations under FEMA (2022) for Indian companies investing abroad; check the current RBI directions.
- **Inbound rules (for entries into India):** the FDI policy and sectoral caps.
- **Public data sources:** national statistics, business registries and licensing authorities, multilateral databases, trade statistics.
- **Tax and transfer pricing:** double-taxation treaties; OECD transfer-pricing guidelines. Seek tax advice.
- **Each stage's skills** list detailed sources.
