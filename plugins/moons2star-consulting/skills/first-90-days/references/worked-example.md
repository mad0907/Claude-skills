# Worked example (illustrative; an external hire as Chief Operating Officer of a ₹3,000 Cr consumer-durables maker; 3 plants, margins down 4 points in 2 years)

**Situation diagnosis:**
- **Operations:** a turnaround (cost, quality complaints up 60%, on-time delivery 78%).
- **R&D and new products:** sustaining success (two strong launches).

**Implications:**
- Move fast on plant performance and quality.
- Learn before touching the R&D processes.

**Expectations agreed with the CEO:**
- Restore 2 points of gross margin within 12 months.
- On-time delivery above 92% in 9 months.
- No change to the product roadmap.
- Monthly one-to-ones.
- Board update at day 90.
- **Resources:** a budget for 2 senior hires.

**Learning agenda:**
- Where exactly is margin lost (materials, yield, freight, warranty)?
- Why did quality fall (supplier change, a new line, skills)?
- What do dealers say?
- How do the plant heads and the union see the issues?

**Listening tour (35 conversations):**
- People: 3 plant heads, the quality head, procurement, the supply-chain team, 10 frontline supervisors, 2 union representatives, 6 dealers, 4 key suppliers, the CFO, the sales head, 2 board members.
- **Five questions:**
  1. What's working well?
  2. What's getting in the way?
  3. What would you change first?
  4. What should I not change?
  5. Who else should I talk to?

**Early wins:**
1. A daily quality-escalation huddle at all plants, with a 48-hour root-cause rule. It is visible, it models a fact-based culture, and it targets complaints.
2. Clear the 6-week backlog of dealer warranty claims, which rebuilds dealer trust.
3. Renegotiate the top 10 freight lanes (a quick ₹8-10 Cr annual saving).

**Team assessment (by day 75):**
- 3 plant heads, plus the heads of quality, procurement and supply chain.
- **Criteria:** competence, judgement, ownership, collaboration.
- Decisions communicated respectfully, with HR.

| Phase | Objectives | Key actions | Milestones |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1-30 | Learn; stabilise quality | Listening tour; plant walks; margin bridge with Finance; launch the quality huddles | Margin bridge done; huddles live at all plants |
| 31-60 | Diagnose; first results | Share the diagnosis with the CEO and team; freight renegotiation; warranty backlog cleared; supplier quality audits | Diagnosis agreed; freight savings contracted |
| 61-90 | Plan and team | 12-month operations plan (yield, procurement, network); team decisions; KPI cadence | Board update; plan approved; team changes announced |

**Operating cadence:**
- Weekly operations review (safety, quality, delivery, cost).
- Monthly plant performance dialogues.
- Quarterly town halls.

**Pitfalls to avoid:**
- Importing the previous employer's lean system wholesale.
- Bypassing plant heads with the union.
- Changing R&D processes.

**Check-ins:** with the CEO at days 30, 60 and 90; a dealer pulse survey at day 75.
