# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Leadership transitions:** situational diagnosis (start-up, turnaround, realignment, sustaining success), securing early wins, aligning expectations and building coalitions, as taught in executive-transition programmes.
- **Stakeholder and coalition mapping:** influence and support analysis.
- **Team assessment:** competence, judgement, energy, focus, relationships and trust as common evaluation dimensions.
- **Organisational culture:** learning culture through listening, artefacts and behaviours.
- **Employment and industrial-relations law:** consult HR and legal on team changes and union engagement (Industrial Relations Code 2020 in India, where applicable).
