# Sources (widely taught concepts; verify regulatory specifics)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Market segmentation:** needs-based, behavioural, value-based and descriptive bases, as taught in marketing-strategy curricula; segment-attractiveness criteria (measurable, accessible, substantial, differentiable, actionable).
- **Customer profitability and cost-to-serve analysis:** management-accounting practice.
- **Clustering and rules-based assignment:** standard analytics methods (k-means, decision trees). Explainability matters for adoption.
- **Fair treatment:** consumer-protection and sector rules (e.g. financial-services conduct expectations) and data-protection law (DPDP Act 2023) on using personal data for segmentation.
