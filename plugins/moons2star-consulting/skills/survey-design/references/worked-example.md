# Worked example (illustrative; customer survey for a B2B logistics software firm deciding where to invest next year)

**Decision:** prioritise investment among onboarding, support, a mobile app and pricing changes.

| Decision question | Table / chart | Survey questions |
|---|---|---|
| Which experiences drive overall satisfaction? | Driver chart (attribute ratings vs overall) | Q3 overall satisfaction; Q5a-f attribute ratings |
| Do SMEs and enterprises differ? | Every table split by segment | S2 company size (screener / quota) |
| How big is mobile demand among field users? | Share using phones at work; feature ranking | Q7, Q8 |
| Is price a churn risk? | Value-for-money by segment; switching consideration | Q9, Q10 |

**Population and frame:** active customer accounts (CRM), one decision-maker and one daily user per account.

**Sampling:** stratified by segment (SME, enterprise) with quotas.

**Target n = 1,000**, so about ±3.1 points overall:
- Each segment needs ≥300 (±5.7 points).
- At an expected 12% response rate, invite about 8,400 contacts.
- Weight to the true segment mix at analysis.

**Questionnaire (excerpt):**
- S1. Do you use [product] at least monthly in your job? (Yes / No → end)
- S2. How many employees does your company have? (<50 / 50-249 / 250-999 / 1,000+)
- Q3. Overall, how satisfied are you with [product]? (Very dissatisfied … Very satisfied, 5 points)
- Q4. Why did you give that rating? (open)
- Q5. How satisfied are you with each of the following? (randomised; 5 points + "not used"): a) setting up / onboarding, b) day-to-day ease of use, c) reporting, d) support response time, e) support quality, f) integrations.
- Q6. Which ONE of these would most improve your experience? (randomised list + other)
- Q7. In the last month, how often did you need [product] while away from a desk? (Never / 1-3 times / weekly / daily)
- Q9. Considering what you pay, how would you rate the value for money? (Very poor … Very good)
- Q10. In the last 6 months, have you seriously considered switching to another provider? (Yes / No / Don't know)

**Fixes made after review:**
- An earlier draft asked "How satisfied are you with our fast and friendly support?" This was leading and double-barrelled, so it was split into Q5d and Q5e with neutral wording.
- "Would you pay more for a mobile app?" was replaced by the behaviour question Q7. Price sensitivity is handled separately.

**Quality controls:** one attention check; flags for responses under 3 minutes and straight-lining in Q5; duplicate-email check.

**Pilot:** 8 cognitive interviews (4 SME, 4 enterprise), then a 5% soft launch.

**Consent:** the purpose, that responses are reported in aggregate only, retention of 12 months, and a contact for data requests.

**Length:** about 8 minutes, tested on mobile.
