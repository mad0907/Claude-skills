# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Questionnaire design:** principles for neutral wording, balanced scales, question order and cognitive pretesting, as taught in survey-methodology curricula.
- **Sampling and precision:** margin of error for proportions (1.96 × √(p(1−p)/n) at 95%); stratified sampling; quotas; weighting.
- **Survey error sources:** coverage, sampling, non-response and measurement error (the total-survey-error framework).
- **Standards:** ISO 20252 (market, opinion and social research); market-research codes of conduct.
- **Data protection:** DPDP Act 2023 (India) and equivalent laws elsewhere; consent and purpose limitation.
