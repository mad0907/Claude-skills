# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Jobs-to-be-done theory:** customers "hire" products to make progress in specific circumstances. A widely taught innovation and marketing concept.
- **Outcome-driven innovation:** job maps (define, locate, prepare, confirm, execute, monitor, modify, conclude), desired-outcome statements, and importance-satisfaction opportunity scoring.
- **Switch interviews and the forces of progress:** push, pull, anxiety and habit as forces affecting switching decisions.
- **Customer research methods:** in-depth interviews about real past behaviour (see `interview-guide`), surveys (see `survey-design`).
