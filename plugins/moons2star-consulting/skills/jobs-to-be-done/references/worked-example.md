# Worked example (illustrative; CFOs of mid-size companies, FP&A, 18 interviews + a 120-respondent survey)

**Core functional job:** "Give the CEO and board a reliable view of next quarter's cash and profit, and explain the variances."
**Related jobs:** prepare the budget; manage lender covenants; support pricing decisions.
**Emotional jobs:** feel confident in the numbers; avoid surprises in board meetings.
**Social jobs:** be seen as a strategic partner, not a scorekeeper.

| Step | Trying to | Desired outcome | Imp. (1-10) | Sat. (1-10) | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Locate | Gather actuals from systems | Minimise the time to consolidate actuals from multiple entities | 8.6 | 4.1 | **13.1** |
| Prepare | Update drivers and assumptions | Minimise the effort to update driver assumptions with business owners | 7.9 | 4.8 | 11.0 |
| Execute | Produce the forecast | Minimise the time to produce a reforecast | 8.1 | 5.5 | 10.7 |
| Confirm | Check the numbers | Minimise the likelihood of formula and link errors | 8.8 | 5.2 | **12.4** |
| Monitor | Explain variances | **Minimise the time to explain variance drivers to the CEO** | 9.1 | 3.6 | **14.6** |
| Conclude | Communicate | Increase the clarity of the board narrative | 8.3 | 5.9 | 10.7 |
| Execute | Scenario analysis | Increase the speed of what-if scenarios during meetings | 7.2 | 3.9 | 10.5 |

(Opportunity = importance + max(importance − satisfaction, 0).)

**Switching forces (from 9 recent switchers):**
- **Push:** a board embarrassment over a variance nobody could explain; analyst turnover.
- **Pull:** driver-based models; automated variance commentary.
- **Anxiety:** "Will the AI explanation be wrong in front of the board?"; implementation effort.
- **Habit:** a 10-year-old spreadsheet model that the CFO trusts.
- **Triggers:** a new CEO or investor; an acquisition; a covenant scare.

**Opportunities:**
| Outcome | Opportunity | Idea | Validation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Explain variances fast (underserved) | Automated driver-based variance bridges, with AI-drafted commentary citing source transactions | "Variance in 10 minutes" feature; CFO review-and-approve flow | Prototype test with 8 CFOs on their last quarter's data |
| Consolidate actuals (underserved) | Pre-built connectors for common ERPs and accounting packages | Connector library + a data-quality checker | Time-to-first-consolidation in a pilot |
| Avoid errors (underserved) | Model-integrity checks | Automatic error and link checks before board packs | Error-detection test on real models |
| Anxiety about AI | Trust features | Every sentence traceable to data; a "confidence" flag | Message test: "every number traceable" |
