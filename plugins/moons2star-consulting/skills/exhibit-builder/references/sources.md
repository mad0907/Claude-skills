# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Graphical perception and information design:** see the `chart-selection` sources.
- **Assertion-evidence slide design:** claim titles supported by visual evidence.

- **Data reconciliation:** standard financial-reporting control practice (tie exhibits back to audited or reported figures).
