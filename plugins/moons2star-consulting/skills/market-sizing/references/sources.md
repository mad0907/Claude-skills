# Sources (public data types and widely taught methods; verify current data)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Methods:** top-down, bottom-up and supply-side triangulation; TAM, SAM and SOM, as taught in strategy and entrepreneurship curricula.
- **Public data sources (India; check the latest releases):** national statistics office surveys (e.g. household consumption expenditure, annual survey of industries, periodic labour force survey); Ministry of Corporate Affairs company data; RBI and sector-regulator statistics; GST and e-invoicing statistics; industry-ministry reports.
- **International:** national statistics agencies; World Bank and IMF databases; UN trade data.
- **Company filings:** annual reports and investor presentations of listed players for supply-side checks.
- **Adoption analogues:** diffusion-of-innovation curves for comparable products and markets.
