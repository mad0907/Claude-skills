# Worked example (illustrative; fictional manufacturer, data-centre consolidation)

**Headline:** consolidate four ageing data centres into one hybrid-cloud platform to avoid a ₹48 Cr refresh and halve outage risk.

- **Situation:** our four regional data centres have run core systems reliably for over a decade.
- **Complication:** three of the four reach end-of-life in 2027, needing ₹48 Cr of hardware refresh. Unplanned outages have doubled in two years (14 in FY26), each costing an estimated ₹35 lakh in lost production.
- **Question:** how do we avoid this spend and reduce outage risk without disrupting plants?
- **Answer:** approve ₹31 Cr to consolidate into one hybrid-cloud platform over 24 months, keeping plant control systems on-site.

**Key-line:**
1. It costs less: 5-year total cost is ₹12 Cr (est.) lower than refreshing, including migration and dual running.
2. It is more resilient: a multi-zone design targets 99.95% availability vs 99.6% today.
3. It is safe for plants: production control systems stay on-site, and cutovers are scheduled in planned shutdowns.
4. Risks are controlled: data stays in Indian regions; workloads are portable to avoid lock-in; there is an exit plan.

**Ordering:** answer-first for the ExCo. For the audit and risk committee, open with the complication (outage trend), then the answer, and lead the key-line with point 4.

**30-second version:** "Three of our four data centres need a ₹48 Cr refresh next year, and outages have doubled. We propose ₹31 Cr to move to one hybrid-cloud platform over two years. It's cheaper over five years, more resilient, and plant systems stay on-site. We're asking for approval today."

1. Exec summary.
