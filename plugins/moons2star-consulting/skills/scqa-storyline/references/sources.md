# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Situation-Complication-Question-Answer (SCQA):** a standard introduction structure taught in executive-writing and consulting communication training.
- **Answer-first (top-down) communication:** pyramid-style structuring of arguments; "bottom line up front" as used in military and government writing guidance.
- **Narrative structure:** classical story arcs (context, tension, resolution) applied to business communication.
- **Plain-language guidance:** public plain-language writing standards (e.g. ISO 24495-1:2023, Plain language: governing principles and guidelines).
