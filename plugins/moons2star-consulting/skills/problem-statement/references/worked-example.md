# Worked example (illustrative; fictional B2B SaaS company, ~2,000 customers)

**Ask (verbatim, from the CEO):** "We need to fix customer churn. Look into it and come back with a plan."
**Trigger:** annual logo churn rose from 8% to 13% in 12 months. The investor board flagged it as a risk to the next funding round.

**First draft (rejected):** "Build a customer-success program to reduce churn." It names a solution.

**Agreed question:** what must the Chief Customer Officer change in onboarding, pricing, product adoption and account coverage to bring annual logo churn back below 9%, and net revenue retention above 105%, within 12 months, without adding net headcount?

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Decision-maker / deadline | CCO, with CEO approval; plan by 15 March for the Q2 budget |
| Symptoms | Churn up 5 pts; 60% of churn among customers under 18 months old; support tickets per account +30% |
| Possible root causes (to test) | Onboarding takes too long (data migration); price rise of 2025 hit small accounts; key features under-adopted; no coverage for mid-market accounts after the sales handover |
| Success criteria | 1. Logo churn ≤9% (rolling 12 months) 2. NRR ≥105% 3. Time-to-first-value ≤30 days. **Guardrail:** CSAT does not fall below 4.2 |
| In scope | Mid-market and SMB segments in India and the Middle East; onboarding, pricing, adoption, coverage model |
| Out of scope | Enterprise segment (separate team, low churn); product roadmap re-prioritisation |
| Parked | A new pricing model for 2028 (revisit after the analysis) |
| Constraints | No net headcount; existing CRM; pricing changes need board approval |
| Stakeholders | CFO (must believe the fix is cost-neutral); CPO (must accept that adoption issues are partly product); Sales (must accept a new handover) |
| Unknowns / hypotheses | H1: Onboarding duration drives early churn. H2: The 2025 price rise caused SMB churn. H3: Accounts without a named success manager churn 2x more |

**Stress test:** answerable in 8 weeks with the existing data ✓ | solution-neutral ✓ | measurable ✓ | owned by the CCO ✓
