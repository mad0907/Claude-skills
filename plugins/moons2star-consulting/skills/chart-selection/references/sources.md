# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Graphical perception research:** the experimentally established ranking of visual encodings (position along a common scale is read most accurately; angle, area and colour saturation least).
- **Information-design principles:** data-to-ink ratio, small multiples, avoiding chart junk; standard in data-visualisation curricula.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 contrast guidance; colour-vision-deficiency-safe palettes; not relying on colour alone.
- **Statistical graphics conventions:** zero baselines for bars, consistent scales, showing uncertainty.
