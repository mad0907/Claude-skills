# Worked examples (illustrative)

**1. Cost per claim across 12 regions vs target**
- **Message:** "Four of 12 regions are above target cost per claim; the East is 28% above."
- **Relationship:** comparison plus deviation from target.
- **Chart:** horizontal bar, sorted high to low, with a vertical target line at ₹1,850.
  *Alternative:* a diverging bar of the % deviation from target (sharper on deviation, but loses the absolute values).
- **Spec:** x-axis ₹ from 0; the 4 regions above target in the accent colour, the rest grey; value labels at bar ends; "Target ₹1,850" labelled on the line.
- **Title:** the message. **Subtitle:** "Average cost per motor claim, H1 FY27, ₹". **Source:** claims system.
- **Avoid:** a map (region size distorts perception); a pie.

**2. Revenue change from FY26 to FY27**
- **Message:** "Price added ₹38 Cr, but volume lost ₹21 Cr and mix lost ₹6 Cr."
- **Chart:** waterfall, from the FY26 bar → price (+) → volume (−) → mix (−) → FY27 bar. Positive steps in one colour, negative in another (shape plus label, not colour alone).

**3. Customer satisfaction vs response time across 40 branches**
- **Message:** "Branches answering within 2 minutes score 15 points higher on satisfaction."
- **Chart:** scatter, with response time on x and satisfaction on y; a trend line with R² noted; the 5 outlier branches labelled.
- **Caveat in the subtitle:** "association, not proof of cause".
