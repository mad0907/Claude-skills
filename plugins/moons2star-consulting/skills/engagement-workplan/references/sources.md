# Sources (widely taught concepts and public standards)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Hypothesis-driven workplanning:** linking analyses to issue-tree leaves and drafting the storyline early, as taught in structured problem-solving and consulting training.
- **Project planning fundamentals:** work breakdown, critical path, buffers and milestone planning (e.g. ISO 21502:2020 guidance on project management; professional project-management bodies' standards).
- **Governance practice:** steering-committee purpose and decision-focused meetings.
- **Data-request management:** standard practice in due diligence and analytics projects (owners, formats, fallbacks).
