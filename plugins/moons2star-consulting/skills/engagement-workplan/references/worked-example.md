# Worked example (illustrative; 6-week pricing review for 3 product lines, team of 4, SteerCos every 2 weeks)

**Key question:** what pricing changes across product lines A, B and C will raise gross margin by ≥2 points within 12 months without losing more than 3% of volume?
**Sponsor:** CCO. **Decision meeting:** ExCo, end of week 6 (pre-read due end of week 5).

| Week | WS1: Price realisation and leakage | WS2: Customer value and willingness to pay | WS3: Competitor and market | Milestones |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Data request; transaction data profiling | Interview guide; book 15 customer interviews | Competitor price scan plan | Kick-off; data request issued (day 2) |
| 2 | Price waterfall by segment | Interviews 1-8 | Price benchmarks A, B | **Ghost deck v1 (end of week 2)**; sponsor check-in |
| 3 | Discount variance analysis | Interviews 9-15; value drivers | Benchmarks C; competitor moves | **SteerCo 1:** test hypotheses; agree price-test scope |
| 4 | Leakage sizing; policy options | Willingness-to-pay synthesis | Elasticity estimates (historic) | Interim findings |
| 5 | Impact model (margin, volume) | Segment price recommendations | Risk of competitor response | **SteerCo 2:** options and trade-offs; pre-read drafted |
| 6 | Final numbers | Rollout and communication plan | — | Pre-wiring (CFO, Head Sales); **ExCo decision** |

**Analysis plan (extract):**
| WS | Analysis | Answers | Data | Exhibit | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Price waterfall (list → invoice → pocket price) | Where is margin leaking? | 24 months of invoice lines, rebates, freight | Waterfall by segment | Analyst 1 | Wk 2 |
| 1 | Discount dispersion vs deal size | Is discounting disciplined? | Same + CRM | Scatter: discount % vs volume | Analyst 1 | Wk 3 |
| 2 | Value-driver interviews | What do customers pay for? | 15 interviews | Value map by segment | Consultant | Wk 4 |
| 3 | Historic elasticity | What volume loss follows price moves? | 3 years of price changes and volumes | Elasticity by line | Analyst 2 | Wk 4 |

**Data request:**
| Item | Owner | Due | Fallback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invoice-level transactions (24 months) | Finance systems | Day 5 | 12-month extract |
| Rebate agreements | Sales ops | Day 7 | Top 50 customers only |
| Freight cost by shipment | Logistics | Day 7 | Average freight per km |

**Critical path:** invoice data by day 5 (owner: Finance systems lead; escalate to the CFO if late).
**Buffer:** 2 days in week 5.

**Risks:**
| Risk | Trigger | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Interview no-shows | <10 interviews booked by week 2 | Sales to escalate; add a short survey |
| Elasticity data too noisy | No usable price changes | Use willingness-to-pay ranges plus a 2-region price test proposal |
