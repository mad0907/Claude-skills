# Worked example (illustrative; screening an investment in Indian cold-chain logistics, 2 weeks)

**Decision:** go or no-go on a detailed diligence of 3 cold-chain operators (investment committee in 2 weeks).

| # | Question | Type | Best sources | Starting searches | Precision | Triangulation | Time | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cold-storage capacity and utilisation by region | Fact / estimate | Agriculture-ministry and horticulture-board statistics; warehousing regulator data; operator filings | "cold storage capacity state-wise [year]"; "cold chain utilisation India" | ±20% | Govt data + sum of top-10 operator capacity | 2 days | Analyst 1 |
| 2 | Demand growth: pharma, dairy, quick commerce, seafood exports | Estimate | Sector-ministry data; export statistics; listed companies' disclosures | "vaccine cold chain capacity India"; "dairy processing growth" | ±25% | 2 sectors bottom-up + industry-body estimate | 3 days | Analyst 2 |
| 3 | Unit economics: revenue per pallet, utilisation, energy cost share | Fact / estimate | Operator filings; 4 expert calls (former operations heads) | — | ±15% | Filings + experts | 3 days | Lead |
| 4 | Regulatory incentives and standards (subsidies, energy, food-safety rules) | Fact | Official scheme guidelines; food-safety regulator | "cold chain subsidy scheme guidelines [year]" | Exact | Official text only | 1 day | Analyst 1 |
| 5 | Competitive intensity and consolidation | Judgement | Experts; filings; deal databases | — | Qualitative | 4 experts + deal history | 2 days | Lead |
| 6 | Key risks (power reliability, technology obsolescence, customer concentration) | Judgement | Experts; operator risk disclosures | — | Qualitative | ≥3 independent views | 1 day | Lead |

**Order of attack:** book expert calls on day 1 (longest lead); Q4 official texts; Q1 and Q2 data; Q3 after the first expert calls.

**Source log format:** claim | value | source | date | definition (e.g. "capacity in million tonnes, multi-commodity") | grade A/B/C | link.

**Ethics:**
- Expert calls through a compliant network.
- No questions about current employers' confidential information.
- Don't reveal the target names.

**Stop rule:** an IC memo with Q1-Q3 at the stated precision by day 9; days 10-11 for synthesis.
