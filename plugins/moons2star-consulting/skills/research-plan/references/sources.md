# Sources (public data types and widely taught practice)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Research design:** linking research questions to decisions; triangulation; primary vs secondary research, as taught in business-research-methods curricula.
- **Official data (India; check the latest):** national statistics office releases; ministry dashboards; regulators (RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, TRAI and others); Ministry of Corporate Affairs filings; trade statistics.
- **International:** national statistics agencies; multilateral databases.
- **Expert-network compliance:** standard practice to avoid material non-public and confidential information; insider-trading regulations (e.g. SEBI PIT Regulations).
- **Data protection and copyright:** DPDP Act 2023 for personal data; licence terms for subscription content.
