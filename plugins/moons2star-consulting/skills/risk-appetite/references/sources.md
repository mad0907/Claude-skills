# Sources (verify current versions)

- **Financial Stability Board: Principles for an Effective Risk Appetite Framework** (Nov 2013): definitions of risk capacity, appetite, limits and profile; roles of the board, CEO, CRO, CFO and internal audit.
- **COSO: Enterprise Risk Management, Integrating with Strategy and Performance** (2017): 5 components and 20 principles; appetite and tolerance in strategy setting.
- **ISO 31000:2018**, Risk management: Guidelines.
- **Companies Act, 2013:** s.134(3)(n) (statement on development and implementation of the risk management policy in the board's report); s.177(4)(vii) (audit committee evaluation of risk management systems); Schedule IV (independent directors to satisfy themselves that risk management systems are robust).
- **SEBI LODR Regulations, 2015:** Reg 17(9) (board responsible for framing, implementing and monitoring the risk management plan); Reg 21 and Schedule II Part C-A (risk management committee: composition, meetings, role including framing the risk management policy and covering cyber and ESG risks). Applicability is the top 1,000 listed entities by market capitalisation. Verify the current text.
- **RBI:** Guidance Note on Operational Risk Management and Operational Resilience (2024); ICAAP and capital-adequacy directions by entity category (scale-based regulation for NBFCs); outsourcing and IT governance directions. Verify the current master directions for your entity type.
- **IRDAI:** corporate governance and risk-management requirements for insurers (master circular). Verify.
- **Basel Committee:** *Principles for the Sound Management of Operational Risk* (revised 2021); *Principles for Operational Resilience* (2021).
