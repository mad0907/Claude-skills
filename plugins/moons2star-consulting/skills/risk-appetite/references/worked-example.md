# Worked example (illustrative; fictional mid-size NBFC, ₹28,000 Cr AUM, growing digital unsecured lending)

## 1. Overall statement
We take credit risk in our chosen segments (MSME secured lending, salaried personal loans) to earn risk-adjusted returns above our cost of equity. We operate with capital and liquidity buffers well above regulatory minimums, so we can withstand a severe-but-plausible stress without raising capital in distress. We have **no appetite** for wilful regulatory breaches, mis-selling, unfair recovery practices, fraud or bribery. We use AI and automation to improve speed and inclusion, but not at the expense of fair treatment or explainable decisions.

## 3. Category appetite (extract)
| Category | Level | Position | Metric | Green | Amber | Red | Current | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Credit: digital unsecured | Open | Grow, but only in segments with proven loss rates | 90+ DPD (12-month-on-book cohort) | <3.0% | 3.0-3.5% | >3.5% | 2.7% | CRO |
| Credit concentration | Cautious | Avoid single-name and sector exposure that a single shock could impair | Top-20 borrowers % of AUM | <8% | 8-10% | >10% | 6.1% | CRO |
| Capital under stress | Minimal | Withstand a severe stress without breaching the buffer | Stressed CRAR (2008-style + 300 bps rate shock) | >17% | 16-17% | <16% | 18.9% | CFO |
| Liquidity | Minimal | Survive 90 days of market closure | Survival horizon (days) | >90 | 60-90 | <60 | 120 | Treasurer |
| Conduct | Averse | Fair treatment in sales and recovery | Complaints per 10k loans (RBI-reportable) | <4 | 4-6 | >6 | 3.2 | CCO |
| Conduct: recovery | Zero (wilful) | No coercive recovery practices | Upheld recovery-conduct complaints | 0 | n/a | ≥1 triggers review | 0 | Head Collections |
| AI and model | Cautious | Automate only explainable, tested decisions | High-tier models without current validation | 0 | 1 | ≥2 | 1 | CRO |
| AI fairness | Cautious | Comparable approval outcomes across groups, after risk factors | Approval-rate ratio, lowest group vs highest (risk-adjusted) | ≥0.85 | 0.80-0.85 | <0.80 | 0.83 | Head Credit Analytics |
| Cyber | Minimal | Protect customer data and critical services | Critical vulnerabilities open >30 days | 0 | 1-3 | >3 | 2 | CISO |
| Operational resilience | Minimal | Loan origination and collections stay within impact tolerance | Unplanned downtime of critical services beyond tolerance (hrs/qtr) | 0 | ≤4 | >4 | 0 | COO |
| Compliance | Zero (wilful) | Full compliance with RBI directions | Overdue regulatory actions | 0 | 1-2 (non-material) | >2 or any material | 1 | CCO |
| ESG: climate | Cautious | Limit exposure to high-transition-risk sectors in MSME book | % of MSME book in high-transition-risk sectors | <12% | 12-15% | >15% | 9% | CRO |

**Plan check:** the FY28 plan grows digital unsecured to 22% of AUM. The modelled 90+ DPD peaks at 3.3% in Q3, **in amber**. → Board discussion: accept, with tighter cut-offs in two sub-segments, or slow growth to 19%.

## 5. Escalation
| Status | Who is told | By when | Required action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amber | Risk owner → CRO; risk management committee at next meeting | 5 working days | Action plan with dates; monitored monthly |
| Red | CRO → RMC Chair and MD immediately; board at next meeting or a special meeting if material | 24 hours | Remediation plan, or a formal time-bound exception approved by the RMC (max 2 quarters), recorded and tracked |
| Repeat amber (3 consecutive months) | Treated as red | — | — |

## 6. Use test
- Product approval: no new product without appetite-fit sign-off by the CRO.
- Pricing: risk-based pricing floors linked to expected loss and the capital charge.
- AI deployment: high-tier models need validation and fairness metrics within appetite before go-live.
- Incentives: sales incentives subject to malus for upheld mis-selling; collections incentives exclude volume-only targets.
