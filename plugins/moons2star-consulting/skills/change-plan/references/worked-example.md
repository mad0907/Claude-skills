# Worked example (illustrative; fictional bank rolling out an AI copilot to 3,000 relationship managers)

**Case for change:**
- Relationship managers (RMs) spend ~40% of their time on notes, proposals and compliance documentation.
- The copilot drafts call notes, proposal sections and product comparisons from approved sources, freeing an estimated 5 hours a week for clients.
- It does not make credit or suitability decisions; RMs remain accountable.
- Not acting means falling behind peers on service speed, and continued compliance-documentation errors (22% of audit findings).

| Group | # | What changes | Impact | Concerns | Key message | Support | Sponsor / manager actions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RMs (affluent) | 2,100 | Notes and proposals drafted by AI; RM reviews | High | "Will AI replace me? Will it be used to monitor me?" | "It takes the paperwork; you keep the relationship. No individual productivity rankings from copilot data" (policy) | 2-hour hands-on training; 120 champions; in-app tips | Regional heads use it in their own reviews; share wins weekly |
| RMs (business banking) | 900 | Same + credit-memo drafting support | High | "Credit memos must be accurate" | "Credit judgement stays yours; drafts cite sources" | Credit-memo clinic; checker guidance | Credit heads co-design templates |
| Team leaders | 260 | Coach usage; review quality | Medium | "More work for me?" | "Fewer rework loops on documentation" | Leader briefing a week before teams; FAQ | Weekly usage and quality huddle |
| Compliance and QA | 40 | New review of AI-assisted documents | Medium | "New risks" | "Every draft cites its source; you get a sampling dashboard" | Early access; co-define the QA checklist | Head of Compliance endorses |
| Employee union representatives | — | Policy on monitoring and roles | — | Job security | Formal briefing; written policy | — | CHRO meeting before launch |

**Sponsor coalition:**
- The Head of Retail Banking announces, explains why, and commits to "no ranking by copilot metrics".
- Regional heads demo it in town halls.

**Communication timeline (extract):**
- Week −4: leaders.
- Week −3: team leaders.
- Week −2: union briefing and policy published.
- Week −1: RM town halls and Q&A.
- Go-live: daily tips.
- Weeks 1-8: pulse survey every 2 weeks; "you said, we did" updates.

**Resistance plan:**
- Fear of monitoring → published policy + union endorsement.
- Accuracy doubts → source citations + champion demos with real cases.
- Low ability in some regions → floor walkers and regional-language guides.

**Reinforcement:**
- Documentation quality added to the QA scorecard.
- Recognition for teams sharing best prompts.
- The old proposal templates retired at week 10.

**Metrics:**
| Type | Metric | Target | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adoption | Weekly active RMs | ≥70% | Week 8 |
| Proficiency | Notes completion time | −40% | Week 8 |
| Outcome | Client meetings per RM per week | +15% | Q2 |
| Quality | Documentation audit findings | −50% | Q3 |
| Sentiment | "Copilot helps me serve clients" | ≥65% agree | Week 6 |
