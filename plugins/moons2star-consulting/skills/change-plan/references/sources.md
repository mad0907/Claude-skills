# Sources (widely taught concepts; verify legal specifics)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Organisational change management:** established models covering the case for change, guiding coalitions, communication, empowerment, short-term wins and anchoring change in culture; individual-transition models (awareness, desire, knowledge, ability, reinforcement). Taught widely in change-management curricula.
- **Research on sponsorship and adoption:** practitioner and academic studies consistently link active, visible sponsorship and manager involvement to successful outcomes.
- **ISO 10018:2020** (quality management: guidance for people engagement) and **ISO 30414** (human-capital reporting) for people metrics.
- **Employment law and consultation:** applicable labour laws and works-council or union consultation requirements (India: Industrial Relations Code 2020 and state rules; verify with HR and counsel).
- **AI at work:** employer guidance on transparency and monitoring; data-protection obligations (DPDP Act 2023) for employee data.
