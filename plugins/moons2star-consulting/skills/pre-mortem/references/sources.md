# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Pre-mortem / prospective hindsight:** a decision-science technique. Research on prospective hindsight finds that imagining an event has already occurred increases the ability to identify reasons for the outcome.
- **Groupthink and debiasing:** independent idea generation (nominal group technique), devil's advocacy, and psychological safety, from organisational-behaviour research.
- **Tripwires and pre-commitment:** decision-making practices that set signals and responses in advance to counter sunk-cost and escalation-of-commitment biases.
- **Risk assessment techniques:** ISO 31010:2019 (e.g. scenario analysis, structured "what-if").
