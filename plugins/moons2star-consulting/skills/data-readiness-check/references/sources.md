# Sources (public standards and laws; verify current versions)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **ISO 8000** (data quality) and **ISO/IEC 25012** (data quality model): quality dimensions and characteristics.
- **ISO/IEC 5259 series** (data quality for analytics and machine learning; parts published 2024-2025).
- **ISO/IEC 42001:2023**, Annex A data-related controls for AI systems.
- **NIST AI RMF 1.0:** MAP and MEASURE functions on data representativeness and bias; **NIST SP 1270** (2022), *Towards a Standard for Identifying and Managing Bias in AI*.
- **India: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** and **DPDP Rules, 2025** (notified 13 Nov 2025; most data-fiduciary obligations from 13 May 2027): notice, consent, purpose, retention, security safeguards, Significant Data Fiduciary duties.
- **EU GDPR** (purpose limitation, data minimisation, DPIAs) where EU personal data is involved; **EU AI Act** Art. 10 data and data-governance requirements for high-risk systems (as amended; verify dates).
- Common ML-practice concepts used here (target leakage, label noise, class imbalance, reject inference, data drift) are standard in data-science curricula.
