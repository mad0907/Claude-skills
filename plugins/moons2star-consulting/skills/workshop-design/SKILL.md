# Workshop Design

*moons2star Consultant's Toolkit · created by Dr. Munmun Das · [moons2star.in](https://moons2star.in)*

**What this gives you:** a session that uses senior people's time to make real decisions, with everyone heard, the hard topics surfaced, and commitments that survive the next Monday.

## The senior standard
1. **Outcomes and decisions first.** Define the 2-4 outputs (decisions, prioritised lists, commitments) before choosing any activity.
2. **Pre-work does the heavy lifting.** Facts, options and data are shared before; the room is for judgement and alignment.
3. **Diverge → converge → decide → commit.** Open up ideas, narrow systematically, decide with an agreed rule, then commit owners and dates.
4. **Decision rights are explicit.** Who decides: the CEO after input, consensus, majority, or consent ("no strong objection")? Agree this upfront.
5. **Design for candour.** Silent individual work before discussion; small groups; anonymous inputs for sensitive topics; a facilitator who manages dominant voices.
6. **Time is the scarcest resource.** Timed agenda; buffers; a parking lot; ruthless prioritisation.
7. **Follow-through is designed in:** actions, owners, dates, a communication plan, and a check-in date.

## Before you start: diagnose
- Purpose and context: why now; the decisions needed; what's been tried.
- Participants: who must be there (decision-makers, implementers, dissenters), number, dynamics, hierarchy.
- Duration, format (in-person, hybrid), location.
- Sensitivities: politics, conflicts, change fatigue, confidentiality.

## Method
1. **Outcomes:** decisions and outputs (e.g. "agree top 3 priorities for next year with owners").
2. **Participants and roles:** sponsor, facilitator, participants, note-taker, subject experts.
3. **Pre-work:** a fact base, options, a pre-survey (anonymous views on key questions), one-page pre-reads.
4. **Agenda flow:**
   - Opening: purpose, outcomes, decision rules, norms.
   - Context: the shared fact base, with brief Q&A.
   - Diverge: idea generation (silent brainstorm, small groups).
   - Converge: clustering, criteria-based scoring, dot voting.
   - Decide: per the decision rule.
   - Commit: owners, dates, resources.
   - Close: summary, communication, check-in.
5. **Methods by purpose:**
   - Ideas: silent writing, then sharing.
   - Prioritisation: criteria scoring, impact-effort matrix.
   - Risks: pre-mortem.
   - Alignment: a "where do we stand" spectrum exercise.
   - Conflict: structured debate with assigned positions.
6. **Timings:** minutes per block; breaks; 10-15% buffer.
7. **Facilitation:**
   - Opening norms.
   - Round-robins; time-boxed speaking.
   - A devil's advocate role.
   - Anonymous polling for sensitive topics.
   - A parking lot.
8. **Materials and set-up:** room layout, wall space, templates, polling tools, hybrid equipment.
9. **Risks and contingencies:** what if the CEO dominates? A key person is absent? Time overruns? Conflict escalates?
10. **Follow-through:** a written summary within 48 hours; an action log; the communication cascade; a 30-day check-in.

## Judgement calls
- **If the decision is already made,** don't fake consultation. Run a "how to implement" session instead.
- **Senior leaders speak last** on contentious topics, to avoid anchoring.
- **Hybrid sessions:** remote participants first in discussions; a digital whiteboard for all; shorter blocks.
- **Conflict is useful** when about ideas; intervene when it becomes personal.
- **Energy management:** hard decisions before lunch; interactive blocks after.

## Red flags
- No defined outcomes; an agenda of presentations.
- Pre-reads at the meeting.
- Decision rule unclear until the end.
- Dominant voices unchecked.
- No owners or dates at the close; no follow-up.

## Variants by context
- **Strategy offsite:** the fact base and strategic options pre-shared; choices and trade-offs; resource allocation.
- **Annual planning:** priorities, targets, budget envelopes, dependencies.
- **Problem-solving workshop:** problem statement, root causes, solution options, a pilot plan.
- **Design sprint:** user problem, sketches, prototype, test plan.
- **Retrospective / lessons learned:** blameless format; what worked, what didn't, what we'll change.
- **Board-management retreat:** strategy, risk appetite, talent; independence norms.

## House rules
- Never invent facts, figures, quotes or sources. Label estimates "(est.)" and list assumptions openly.
- If key inputs are missing, ask up to 3 short questions, or proceed with clearly stated assumptions if the user wants speed.
- Answer first, then support. Plain English; define any jargon.
- Use the user's currency, units, terminology and context (e.g. ₹ and lakh/crore for Indian users).
- Do calculations with code or a spreadsheet when available, not mental arithmetic.
- For legal, regulatory, tax or accounting specifics, flag them for verification against current official sources and qualified advisers.
- Remind users not to paste confidential or personal data they are not permitted to share; suggest anonymising.
