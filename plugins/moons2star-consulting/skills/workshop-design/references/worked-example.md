# Worked example (illustrative; a 1.5-day strategy offsite for the 12-member leadership team of an NBFC; ₹18,000 Cr AUM)

**Outcomes:**
1. Agree the 3 strategic priorities for the next 3 years.
2. Agree which 2 of 5 candidate initiatives to stop or defer.
3. Assign owners and 100-day milestones.
4. Agree the board messaging.

**Participants:**
- The CEO (sponsor and final decision-maker).
- 11 leadership-team members.
- An external facilitator.
- A note-taker from the strategy office.
- The CRO as the formal "risk challenger".

**Decision rule:** the CEO decides after structured input. Priorities need no strong objection from the CRO or CFO on risk or capital grounds; objections are recorded.

**Pre-work (due 1 week before):**
- A 12-page fact base: performance, peers, customer trends, regulation, capital.
- 5 initiative one-pagers (with value, cost, risk).
- An anonymous pre-survey: top priorities, and what we should stop.

| Time | Block | Purpose | Method | Output | Lead |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** 9:00-9:30 | Opening | Outcomes, decision rule, norms | Sponsor framing | Shared contract | CEO / facilitator |
| 9:30-10:30 | Fact base | Shared reality | Pre-read Q&A only (no presenting); pre-survey results shown | Agreed facts; open questions | Strategy head |
| 10:45-12:15 | Diverge | Future priorities | Silent individual writing (10 min), then 3 small groups | 15-20 candidate priorities | Facilitator |
| 13:00-14:30 | Converge | Narrow down | Clustering; scoring on value, capability fit, risk, capital need | Top 6 | Facilitator |
| 14:45-16:15 | Challenge | Stress-test the top 6 | Pre-mortem per priority; CRO challenge | Risks; refined list | CRO |
| 16:30-17:30 | Initiative stop / defer | Free resources | Impact-effort plot of 5 initiatives; anonymous poll | Proposed 2 to stop | Facilitator |
| **Day 2** 9:00-10:30 | Decide | Final 3 priorities and 2 stops | Positions stated (senior members last); CEO decides; objections recorded | Decisions | CEO |
| 10:45-12:00 | Commit | Owners, 100-day milestones, resources | Template per priority | Commitments | Owners |
| 12:00-12:30 | Close | Board messaging, communication cascade | Draft key messages | Communication plan | CEO |

**Buffer:** 15 minutes built into each half-day.

**Facilitation notes:**
- Phones away except during breaks.
- Round-robins on contentious items.
- The CEO speaks last in the decide block.
- A parking lot for operational issues.

**Risks and contingencies:**
- **The debate overruns:** drop the board-messaging block (do it in the following week).
- **Conflict between Retail and SME heads over capital:** structured debate with criteria.
- **A key member is absent:** their delegate gets a pre-briefing.

**Follow-through:**
- Written decisions within 48 hours.
- An action log in the monthly leadership review.
- Cascade to the top 100 managers within 2 weeks.
- A 30-day check-in.
