# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Facilitation practice:** diverge-converge structures, silent brainstorming, nominal group technique, dot voting, as taught in facilitation and design-thinking curricula.
- **Group decision-making:** decision rules (consensus, consent, consultative, majority); avoiding groupthink and anchoring.
- **Psychological safety:** creating conditions for candour in teams (organisational-behaviour research).
- **Design sprints and retrospectives:** time-boxed, structured team methods from product development and agile practice.
- **Pre-mortems:** see the `pre-mortem` skill.
