# Sources (public data types; verify current versions)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **PESTEL / PEST analysis:** a standard environmental-scanning framework in strategy curricula.
- **Official sources (India; check the latest):** Gazette notifications; ministry and regulator websites (e.g. pharmaceuticals, environment, labour, finance); state industrial policies and pollution-control board conditions; RBI publications; national statistics releases.
- **International:** central-bank and statistics-agency data; multilateral organisations' outlooks; trade-policy announcements.
- **Climate and environment:** national and state climate action plans; climate scenario sets used for disclosure (IFRS S2).
- **Horizon scanning practice:** government foresight toolkits; signposts and indicators.
