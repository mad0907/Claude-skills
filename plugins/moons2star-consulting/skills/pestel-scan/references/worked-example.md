**Top factors → implications:**
1. **Effluent and ZLD:** design ZLD from day 1 (₹45-60 Cr capex est.) — EHS Head. Signpost: consent-to-establish conditions.
2. **GMP (Schedule M and export inspections):** build the quality system to the strictest export standard; a data-integrity programme — Quality Head. Signpost: inspection observations in the cluster.
3. **PLI eligibility:** apply in the current window; align the product list — Strategy. Signpost: approval letter.
4. **Talent:** an early hiring and training academy; retention plans — CHRO. Signpost: QA attrition >15%.
5. **China+1 demand:** secure 2 anchor customer agreements before commissioning — Commercial. Signpost: signed agreements.

**Handoffs:**
- **Scenarios:** buyer diversification speed × incentive continuity.
- **Risk register:** effluent, inspection outcomes, FX.
- **ESG:** water and effluent.
