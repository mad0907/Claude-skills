# Worked example (illustrative; fictional ₹800 Cr specialty-chemicals company selling mainly to textile makers in India)

**Target:** ₹1,500 Cr revenue by FY31.
**Momentum case:**
- Textile chemicals grow ~6%/yr, but share erodes from Chinese imports (−1 pt/yr).
- Momentum ≈ ₹950 Cr by FY31.
- **Gap = ₹550 Cr.** The risk-weighted plan should cover ~₹650 Cr (a ~20% buffer).

| Option | Quadrant | Horizon | Distance | Route | Revenue FY31 | EBITDA margin | Investment | Time | P(success) | Risk-weighted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Key-account programme + technical service for top 50 textile mills | Penetration | H1 | 0 | Organic | ₹90 Cr | 18% | ₹15 Cr | 1-2 yrs | 0.8 | ₹72 Cr |
| Value pricing on specialty finishes (vs cost-plus) | Penetration | H1 | 0 | Organic | ₹40 Cr | (margin) | ₹3 Cr | <1 yr | 0.7 | ₹28 Cr |
| Exports to Bangladesh and Vietnam textile hubs | Market dev. | H1-H2 | 1 (geography) | Distributor partners | ₹150 Cr | 15% | ₹25 Cr | 2-3 yrs | 0.6 | ₹90 Cr |
| Technical-textile chemicals (coatings for PPE, filtration) | Product dev. | H2 | 1 (product) | Organic + licensing | ₹180 Cr | 22% | ₹70 Cr | 2-4 yrs | 0.55 | ₹99 Cr |
| Leather and paper chemicals (similar chemistries) | Product + market | H2 | 2 | **Acquisition** of a ₹200 Cr player | ₹230 Cr | 16% | ₹350 Cr | 1-2 yrs post-deal | 0.6 | ₹138 Cr |
| Sustainable / bio-based dyes and auxiliaries for export brands | Product dev. | H2-H3 | 2 (tech, customers) | Partnership with a research institute | ₹120 Cr | 25% | ₹40 Cr | 3-5 yrs | 0.4 | ₹48 Cr |
| Water-treatment chemicals for industrial clusters | Diversification | H3 | 3 | Joint venture | ₹150 Cr | 14% | ₹60 Cr | 4+ yrs | 0.3 | ₹45 Cr |
| Digital colour-matching service for mills (AI-assisted) | Business model | H2 | 1 | Organic | ₹30 Cr + pull-through | 30% | ₹12 Cr | 2-3 yrs | 0.5 | ₹15 Cr |

**Recommended portfolio:**
- **H1:** key accounts, value pricing, exports.
- **H2:** technical textiles, the leather/paper acquisition, digital colour-matching.
- **H3 option:** bio-based dyes (small funded option).
- Risk-weighted total ≈ ₹490 Cr. **Short of the ₹650 Cr goal.**

**Choices:**
- (a) Accept a revised target of ~₹1,350-1,400 Cr; or
- (b) add a second acquisition in H2 (raises capital need and integration risk); or
- (c) accelerate exports with a Southeast Asia technical centre.

**Recommendation:** (a) plus (c) now; keep (b) as a contingent option.

**Capacity check:** one acquisition plus one new product platform at a time. The water-treatment JV is parked.

**Key bets and signposts:**
| Bet | Leading indicator | Decision point |
|---|---|---|
| Technical textiles | Qualified at 5 converters by Q4 FY27 | Scale plant investment, or stay toll-manufactured |
| Acquisition | Synergy capture ≥70% of plan by month 18 | Integration review at month 12 |
| Exports | Distributor sell-out ≥₹40 Cr by FY28 | Open a Southeast Asia technical centre |
