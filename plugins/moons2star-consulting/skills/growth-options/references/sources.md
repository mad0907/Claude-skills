# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Product-market growth matrix:** market penetration, market development, product development, diversification. A standard strategy-curriculum tool.
- **Three horizons of growth:** balancing core (H1), emerging (H2) and options (H3) businesses.
- **Adjacency strategy:** evidence that success rates decline with the number of dimensions that change (customer, product, channel, geography, capability).
- **Gap analysis and momentum case:** standard strategic-planning practice.
- **Real options thinking:** small, staged investments to preserve the right to scale later.
- **M&A success factors:** synergy realism, integration planning, and the risk of paying for growth. Covered in standard corporate-finance and strategy texts.
