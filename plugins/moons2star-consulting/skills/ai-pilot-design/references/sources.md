# Sources (public standards and widely taught methods; verify current versions)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Experimental design:** randomised controlled trials, matched controls and difference-in-differences, as used in public policy and product experimentation; basic power and sample-size calculations for proportions and means.
- **NIST AI RMF 1.0** (MEASURE and MANAGE) and **NIST AI 600-1 GenAI Profile:** test, evaluation, verification and validation (TEVV) expectations; GenAI risks such as confabulation and information security.
- **ISO/IEC 42001:2023:** AI system lifecycle controls, including verification and validation before deployment.
- **OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (2025):** risks to test in GenAI pilots (prompt injection, sensitive information disclosure, excessive agency, misinformation).
- **India:** DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025 for personal data in pilots; RBI expectations on customer grievance handling, and the FREE-AI recommendations for regulated entities (verify any directions issued).
- **EU AI Act** Art. 50 transparency for AI interacting with people, where in scope (verify dates after the 2026 Omnibus).
