# Sources (widely taught concepts and laws; verify current versions)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Value-based pricing and economic value to the customer:** pricing-strategy curricula (next-best alternative plus differentiation value).
- **Pocket-price waterfall and price-realisation analysis:** standard pricing-practice tools to identify leakage between list and net price.
- **Willingness-to-pay methods:** the price sensitivity meter (four-question survey), conjoint analysis, discrete-choice experiments, and in-market tests. Stated intent overstates actual purchase.
- **Price elasticity and break-even volume analysis:** managerial-economics fundamentals.
- **Competition law:** Competition Act, 2002 (India) and equivalents. No price coordination or information exchange with competitors.
- **Consumer protection:** price transparency and unfair-trade-practice rules (e.g. Consumer Protection Act, 2019 in India); sector price controls where applicable.
