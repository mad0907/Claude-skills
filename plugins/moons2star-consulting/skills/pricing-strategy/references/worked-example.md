# Worked example (illustrative; HR software sold at a flat ₹500 per user per month, falling margins, large customers getting 40% discounts)

**Objectives:** restore gross margin from 61% to 68% within 12 months; reduce discount dispersion; keep churn below 10%.

**Anchors:**
- **Value to customer:** payroll and compliance automation saves ~₹1,400 per employee per year in HR effort and avoids penalties (est. from 12 customer studies).
- **Cost floor:** ₹170 per user per month (hosting, support, payroll-compliance updates).
- **Competitors:** ₹350-₹700 per user per month, depending on modules.

**Price metric:** per active employee per month, with module-based tiers, because value scales with headcount and module use.

| Tier | Segment | Includes | Price (per employee per month) | Value story |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Essentials | Small firms (<200) | Payroll, compliance, self-service | ₹350 | "Compliant payroll, zero penalties" |
| Professional | Mid-market (200-2,000) | + attendance, performance, analytics | ₹550 | "One system for the employee life-cycle" |
| Enterprise | 2,000+ | + integrations, audit trails, dedicated success manager, SLAs | ₹700 list; volume bands | "Control and scale" |

**Realisation analysis (last 12 months):**
- Average discount 24%; dispersion is high.
- 40% discounts were given on deals as small as 50 seats. Discount correlation with deal size is only 0.18.
- Estimated leakage vs the policy proposed below: ₹6.2 Cr a year.

**Discount governance:**
| Deal size | Max discount | Approver | Give-get |
|---|---|---|---|
| <200 seats | 5% | Sales manager | Annual prepayment |
| 200-2,000 | 15% | Regional head | 2-year term |
| >2,000 | 25% | CFO (deal desk) | 3-year term + reference + case study |

**Willingness to pay:**
- Price-sensitivity survey (n=180): acceptable range ₹420-₹640 for the Professional tier.
- Win/loss: price was the main loss reason in only 18% of losses.

**Impact (computed with code; illustrative):**
- Mix and discount changes raise average realised price from ₹380 to ₹445 (+17%).
- Contribution margin is ~55% before the change.
- Break-even volume loss = 0.17 ÷ (0.55 + 0.17) ≈ 24%. Expected churn impact is 3-5 points (est.).
- Gross margin reaches ~67-69%.

**Rollout:**
- New pricing for new customers from 1 April.
- Existing customers move at renewal, with a 12-month cap of +12%.
- Value-review meetings for the top 100 accounts.
- Sales incentives move to 60% revenue and 40% margin.

**Legal:** no coordination with competitors; clear price disclosure in contracts.
**KPIs:** realised price, discount dispersion, win rate by tier, net revenue retention, churn.
