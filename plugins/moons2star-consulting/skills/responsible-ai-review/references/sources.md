# Sources (public standards and laws; as researched November 2026; verify)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **NIST AI RMF 1.0** (2023): trustworthiness characteristics (valid and reliable, safe, secure and resilient, accountable and transparent, explainable and interpretable, privacy-enhanced, fair with harmful bias managed). **NIST AI 600-1 GenAI Profile** (2024). **NIST SP 1270** (bias).
- **ISO/IEC 42005:2025** (AI system impact assessment); **ISO/IEC 42001:2023** (AI management system); **ISO/IEC 23894:2023** (AI risk management); **ISO/IEC TR 24027** (bias in AI systems).
- **OECD AI Principles** (2019, updated 2024); **UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI** (2021).
- **EU AI Act** (Reg. 2024/1689, as amended by Reg. 2026/1744): Art. 9-15 requirements for high-risk systems (risk management, data, documentation, transparency, human oversight, accuracy and robustness); Art. 27 fundamental-rights impact assessment for certain deployers; Art. 50 transparency. Annex III obligations from 2 Dec 2027 (verify).
- **India:** DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025 (most obligations from 13 May 2027; Significant Data Fiduciary algorithmic due diligence); MeitY India AI Governance Guidelines (Nov 2025); RBI FREE-AI report (Aug 2025) for regulated entities; sector regulators' consumer-protection and grievance rules.
- **OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (2025):** security testing scope for GenAI.
- **Fairness metrics** (selection-rate ratio, equalised odds, calibration) are standard in the algorithmic-fairness literature. Note that metrics can conflict, so choose them based on the harm in context and document why.
