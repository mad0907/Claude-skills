# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Red teaming and structured challenge:** devil's advocacy, steelmanning, and alternative-analysis techniques used in strategy, military and intelligence analysis.
- **Base rates and the outside view:** reference-class forecasting; typical cost overruns, schedule slips and synergy realisation in large projects and deals.
- **Cognitive biases in decisions:** groupthink, confirmation bias, optimism bias, sunk-cost effects (behavioural decision research).
- **Board oversight:** directors' duty of care and constructive challenge (Companies Act 2013, Section 166 in India; corporate governance codes).
- **Competition law:** merger control thresholds (Competition Act 2002 in India; check the current deal-value thresholds).
