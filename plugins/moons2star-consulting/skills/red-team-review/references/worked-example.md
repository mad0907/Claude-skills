# Worked example (illustrative; a proposal to acquire a regional dairy brand for ₹1,200 Cr to enter South India)

**Steelman:**
- The acquisition gives immediate access to a trusted regional brand, 9,000 village collection points and 40,000 retail outlets in a region where building organically would take 6-8 years.
- The price of ~1.2x revenue is in line with past regional dairy deals.
- The plan targets ₹180 Cr of annual synergies by year 3.

**Verdict:** survives with fixes, but the synergy case and the farmer-relationship risk need rework before the board.

| # | Issue | Category | Severity | Why it matters | Fix or test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Synergies of ₹180 Cr (18% of the target's ~₹1,000 Cr revenue) are above typical realisation; 60% come from "procurement price harmonisation" with farmers | Assumption / response | **Fatal** | Cutting farmer prices risks supply loss to cooperatives and reputational damage; the value case depends on it | Rebuild synergies excluding farmer-price cuts; test the case at 50% synergy realisation; show IRR |
| 2 | Volume growth of 14% a year assumed vs 7-9% category growth in the region | Assumption | Major | Requires share gains against strong cooperatives | Base-case growth at category growth; share gains as upside with named levers |
| 3 | Integration of the cold chain and IT within 12 months | Execution | Major | Comparable integrations took 18-30 months; dual running costs not budgeted | 24-month plan; add ₹35 Cr dual-running cost |
| 4 | Promoter family's role after the deal is unclear | Execution / response | Major | Brand trust and farmer relations are personal to the family | Retention and earn-out structure for 2 key family executives |
| 5 | The competition-law filing threshold has not been assessed | Legal | Major | Deal-value thresholds may require approval; timeline risk | Legal opinion on combination filing |
| 6 | No alternative considered (a JV, or building one state first) | Option | Minor-Major | Staging could cut risk at a lower price | Add a staged JV option to the paper with comparative value and risk |
| 7 | Brand health data is 3 years old | Evidence | Minor | Brand strength may have shifted | A fresh brand study before signing |

**This plan fails if:** farmers defect after price harmonisation; cooperatives respond with price and procurement wars; or integration delays erode service to retailers.

**Questions before the decision:**
- What is the deal IRR without farmer-price synergies?
- What is our farmer-retention plan?
- What happens if a large cooperative matches our procurement prices?

**What's right (keep):** strategic logic of buying distribution and trust; the reasonable entry price; the strong retail-coverage data.
