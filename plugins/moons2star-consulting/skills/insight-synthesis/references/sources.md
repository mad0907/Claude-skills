# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **"What, so what, now what":** a reflective-analysis structure widely used in management education and analytics.
- **Root-cause analysis:** "5 whys" and cause-and-effect techniques from quality management.
- **Causal inference basics:** correlation vs causation, confounders, experiments and quasi-experiments, as taught in statistics and analytics curricula.
- **Analytical storytelling:** turning findings into decision-oriented messages (see `scqa-storyline` and `action-titles`).
