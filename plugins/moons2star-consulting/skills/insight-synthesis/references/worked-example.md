# Worked example (illustrative; fictional FMCG company, quarterly sales review with 15 observations)

**Observations (extract):**
1. West region sales −6% QoQ.
2. Two West distributors' orders −40%.
3. Those distributors' credit lines were reduced in July.
4. Modern trade +12%.
5. E-commerce +25%, but returns rose to 9%.
6. Premium SKU share +3 pts.
7. Rural general trade flat.
8. Promotions spend +18%.
9. Promotion ROI below 1.0 on 40% of schemes.
…

| # | Insight headline | What | So what | Now what | Confidence | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | West's 6% decline comes entirely from two distributors whose credit was cut, not from consumer demand | Obs 1-3; retail offtake data in West flat | ~₹11 Cr per quarter of lost sell-in; shelves at risk of stock-outs | Restore credit with security (bank guarantee) or appoint an interim distributor | High (offtake data confirms demand) | — |
| 2 | 40% of promotion schemes lose money; reallocating them would fund growth without extra spend | Obs 8-9; scheme-level ROI analysis | ₹14 Cr of the ₹35 Cr promotion budget has ROI < 1.0 | Stop or redesign 18 schemes; shift funds to modern-trade visibility (ROI 1.6) | Medium (ROI method excludes halo effects) | Holdout test in 2 states |
| 3 | E-commerce growth is real, but returns are eroding a third of its margin gain | Obs 5; returns by SKU | 9% returns cost ~₹4 Cr per quarter; concentrated in 3 SKUs with packaging damage | Fix packaging on the 3 SKUs; returns target 5% | High | — |
| 4 | Premiumisation is working in metros and can extend to Tier-2 modern trade | Obs 4, 6; Tier-2 basket data | Premium margin is 8 pts higher; Tier-2 modern-trade premium share is half the metro level | Pilot a premium range in 300 Tier-2 stores | Medium | Pilot |

**Parked:** rural flat (in line with the category; no specific action); new-SKU launches on plan.
