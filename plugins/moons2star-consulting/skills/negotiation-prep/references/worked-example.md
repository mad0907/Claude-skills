# Worked example (illustrative; a mid-sized retailer renegotiating a 3-year cloud and IT-managed-services contract, current spend ₹48 Cr a year)

**Our interests (ranked):**
1. Cut the run cost by ≥15%.
2. Improve uptime during sale events.
3. Flexibility to move workloads (avoid lock-in).
4. Stable account team.

**Their interests (estimated):**
- Keep a marquee retail reference.
- Multi-year revenue visibility.
- Expand into data and analytics services.
- Protect margin.

**BATNA:**
- **Ours:** a credible offer from an alternative provider at ₹41 Cr a year, plus a one-time migration cost of ₹9 Cr and 6 months of transition risk. Spread over 3 years, that is about ₹44 Cr a year all-in.
- **Theirs:** losing ₹48 Cr a year of revenue and a reference customer; their pipeline to replace it is uncertain.
- **Strengthen ours:** complete a migration-readiness assessment, and get the alternative's offer in writing.

**Numbers (annual):**
- **Reservation:** ₹44 Cr. Above this, the switch is cheaper.
- **Target:** ₹40 Cr (−17%), justified by benchmark rates for comparable workloads and our volume growth.
- **Opening:** ₹37.5 Cr, citing the benchmark range and committed growth.

**ZOPA (estimated):** their likely floor is ~₹39-40 Cr given margin norms, so the zone is about ₹39-44 Cr.

| Issue | Value to us | Value to them | Trade idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual price | High | High | Core |
| Contract term (3 → 5 years) | Low-medium (if there's a price-review clause) | High | Give 5 years in exchange for price and an annual benchmarking right |
| Sale-event uptime SLA (99.95%) with service credits | High | Medium (cost to them) | Ask; offer an advance capacity forecast |
| Data-portability and exit-assistance clause | High | Low-medium | Must-have; low cost to them |
| Analytics pilot (paid, ₹2 Cr) | Medium | High | Offer in exchange for the price target |
| Case study / reference | Low | High | Give in exchange for SLA credits |
| Payment terms (60 → 45 days) | Low | Medium | Concede late if needed |

**Creative options:** volume-tiered pricing; a gain-share on savings from optimisation; a 2-year price lock with benchmarking in year 3.

**Objective standards:** published rate cards for comparable services; an independent benchmarking study; our historical utilisation data.

**Concession plan:**
1. Term extension.
2. Reference rights.
3. Analytics pilot.
4. Payment terms.

Each is given only in exchange for movement on price, the SLA or the exit clause.

**Team and authority:** CIO leads; CFO approves the final price (authority up to ₹42 Cr); procurement drafts; legal reviews the exit and liability terms.

**Process:**
- Share the benchmark and our growth plan early; don't reveal the alternative's exact price.
- **On deadlock:** reframe to total value (SLA plus pilot), or escalate to the provider's regional head.

**Walk-away rule:** if total annual cost exceeds ₹44 Cr, or the exit-assistance clause is refused, proceed with the alternative provider.
