# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Principled negotiation:** interests vs positions, BATNA, objective criteria, options for mutual gain, as taught in negotiation curricula.
- **Distributive concepts:** reservation point, target, anchoring, the zone of possible agreement (ZOPA).
- **Integrative bargaining:** trading across issues with different valuations; contingent agreements.
- **Cross-cultural negotiation:** communication context, hierarchy and relationship norms.
- **Legal limits:** competition law on information exchange (Competition Act 2002 in India); contract law; public procurement rules where the counterparty is government.
