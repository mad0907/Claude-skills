# Sources and further reading (verify current versions)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Multi-criteria decision analysis (MCDA):** weighted-sum scoring with anchored scales, as described in public-sector appraisal guidance (e.g. UK HM Treasury *Green Book* on options appraisal).
- **EU AI Act** (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689): Art. 5 prohibited practices; Annex III high-risk areas. As amended by the 2026 Digital Omnibus, Annex III obligations apply from 2 Dec 2027 (verify). Use these to define the risk gate where in scope.
- **NIST AI RMF 1.0** and **NIST AI 600-1 Generative AI Profile:** risk considerations to inform the gate.
- **India:** RBI FREE-AI Committee report (Aug 2025) for regulated entities; MeitY India AI Governance Guidelines (Nov 2025); DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025 (most obligations from 13 May 2027).
- **Portfolio horizons:** the widely used "three horizons" idea of balancing core, adjacent and transformational bets.
- **Internal evidence:** your AI risk register and data-readiness results are the best inputs for gate and feasibility scores.
