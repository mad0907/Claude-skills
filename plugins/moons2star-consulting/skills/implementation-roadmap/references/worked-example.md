# Worked example (illustrative; fictional manufacturer, 18-month finance shared-services centre in Pune)

**End state:** transactional finance for 6 business units (AP, AR, general accounting, T&E, fixed assets) delivered from Pune with standard processes on one ERP instance.
**Outcome KPIs:**
- Cost per invoice −35%.
- Close in 6 days (from 9).
- First-time-right ≥97%.
- BU satisfaction ≥4.0/5.

| Workstream (owner) | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 | Q5 | Q6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Site and people (CHRO) | Lease, leadership hires | Hire wave 1 (40) | Hire wave 2 (35) | Training academy | Hire wave 3 (20) | Stabilise |
| Process (Group Controller) | Standard process design | Wave 1 transition (Services, Mfg AP) | Hypercare | Wave 2 (Retail, AR) | Wave 3 (GL, fixed assets) | Continuous improvement |
| Technology (CIO) | ERP harmonisation design | Workflow + document capture live | ERP config for wave 2 | Legacy decommission (BU 1-2) | Wave 3 config | Decommission complete |
| Controls and risk (CRO) | Control design; segregation-of-duties matrix | Transition control testing | Internal audit review wave 1 | — | Internal audit review wave 2-3 | Annual review |
| Change and comms (Programme Director) | Stakeholder map; town halls | BU readiness checks | Wave 2 readiness | — | Wave 3 readiness | Handover to BAU |

**Early wins (≤90 days):**
- Duplicate-payment check across all BUs (₹1.2 Cr recovered) — Controller.
- A single vendor-onboarding portal — CIO.

**Key milestones:**
- Q2: wave 1 at ≥95% first-time-right for 2 consecutive closes.
- Q4: 60% of volume in Pune.
- Q6: close in 6 days, achieved twice.

**Critical path:** ERP harmonisation for the Retail BU (Q3), since wave 2 depends on it.
**Capacity flag:** the Retail BU is also running a POS upgrade in Q3-Q4, so its wave is moved to Q4.

**Stage gates:**
| Gate | Date | Criteria | Decision-maker | Options |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1: Wave 2 go | End Q3 | Wave 1 first-time-right ≥95%; backlog ≤2 days; BU sign-off | SteerCo | Go / delay one quarter / re-scope |
| G2: Wave 3 go | End Q5 | Waves 1-2 KPIs met; legacy decommission on track | SteerCo + CFO | Go / partial |

**Governance:**
- Monthly SteerCo, chaired by the Group CFO.
- Weekly workstream leads meeting.
- Quarterly Audit Committee update on controls.

**Deferred:** treasury and tax processes (to a phase 2 decision in Q6).
