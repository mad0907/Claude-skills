# Sources (public standards and widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **ISO 21502:2020** (guidance on project management) and **ISO 21503** (programme management): planning, governance, stage gates, benefits.
- **Stage-gate governance:** phase-gate decision practices used in capital projects and product development.
- **Critical-path and dependency management:** project-scheduling fundamentals.
- **Benefits-led programme management:** public-sector programme guidance (outcome-based milestones, benefits realisation).
- **Change integration:** see `change-plan`; organisational-change practice of embedding change activities in each workstream.
