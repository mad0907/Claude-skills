# Implementation Roadmap

*moons2star Consultant's Toolkit · created by Dr. Munmun Das · [moons2star.in](https://moons2star.in)*

**What this gives you:** a roadmap that leaders can steer and teams can execute. It is clear about outcomes, sequencing and dependencies, realistic about capacity, and has gates where evidence decides whether to continue.

## The senior standard
1. **Outcomes before activities.** Define the end state and 3-5 measurable outcomes; milestones are evidence of progress toward them ("40% of invoices touchless"), not activities ("system configured").
2. **Sequence by dependency and learning.** Foundations (data, platforms, policies, people) come early; scale follows proof.
3. **Early wins matter.** Visible, valued results within 90 days build credibility and funding; choose ones aligned with the end state.
4. **Capacity is the binding constraint.** The same experts, managers and change-weary teams are usually overloaded. Plan per quarter; limit concurrent initiatives.
5. **Stage gates with teeth.** Each gate has criteria, evidence and a decision-maker; options include continue, adjust, pause or stop.
6. **One accountable owner per workstream,** with clear decision rights and escalation paths.
7. **Living plan.** Re-plan quarterly on evidence; keep a change log.

## Before you start: diagnose
- What was approved (scope, budget, benefits, timeline) and what's non-negotiable.
- Current-state baseline; known constraints (systems, regulation, people, contracts).
- Teams, partners and capacity; other programmes competing for the same people.
- Governance expectations (board reporting, SteerCo).

## Method
1. **End state and outcomes:** describe the target state; define 3-5 outcome KPIs with targets and dates.
2. **Workstreams (4-7):** e.g. process, technology, data, people and change, commercial, governance and risk. Name the owners.
3. **Initiatives per workstream:** scope, outputs, outcome contribution, dependencies, effort, cost.
4. **Phasing:**
   - **Mobilise (0-3 months):** team, governance, baseline, quick wins, detailed design.
   - **Foundations (3-9):** platforms, data, policies, pilots.
   - **Scale (9-18):** roll-out by wave (region, unit, product).
   - **Embed (18+):** decommission legacy, institutionalise, transfer to business-as-usual.
5. **Early wins:** 2-4 within 90 days, each with an owner, a metric and a communication plan.
6. **Dependencies and critical path:** identify cross-workstream dependencies and external ones (vendors, regulators); highlight the critical path and the float.
7. **Capacity and budget:** quarterly view of key roles and spend vs approved; resolve overloads by resequencing.
8. **Stage gates:** criteria (outcomes, readiness, risk, budget), evidence and decision-maker per gate; define pivot and stop options.
9. **Governance:** SteerCo cadence and decisions; workstream rhythm; escalation thresholds; reporting to the board.
10. **Risks:** top roadmap risks and mitigations (see `risk-assessment`, `pre-mortem`).
11. **Visualise:** a table, plus a simple text timeline by quarter.

## Judgement calls
- **Waves beat big bangs** for most organisational and technology change. Choose the first wave for learning (representative but forgiving).
- **Decommissioning is where benefits hide.** Plan legacy shutdown explicitly, or costs persist.
- **Don't let the roadmap become a wish list.** If capacity doesn't fit, drop or defer. Say what's not happening.
- **Integrate change management** into every workstream, not as a separate afterthought.

## Red flags
- Milestones that are activities with no evidence of outcome.
- Everything starting in month 1.
- No owners, or committees as owners.
- No gates; funding fully released upfront.
- Dependencies discovered during execution.
- No decommissioning plan.

## Variants by context
- **Technology programmes (ERP, cloud, data):** design, build, test, migrate and cutover phases; data migration and testing as the critical path; hypercare.
- **Cost transformation:** quick wins first; structural levers later; savings tracking (see `cost-takeout`).
- **AI programmes:** foundations (data, platform, governance) in parallel with wave-1 pilots; scale by use-case family (see `playbook-ai-roadmap`).
- **GCC set-up:** entity and site; leadership hires; process transitions by wave; knowledge transfer and stabilisation.
- **M&A integration:** day 1, 100 days, synergy waves; clean-team rules before close.

## House rules
- Never invent facts, figures, quotes or sources. Label estimates "(est.)" and list assumptions openly.
- If key inputs are missing, ask up to 3 short questions, or proceed with clearly stated assumptions if the user wants speed.
- Answer first, then support. Plain English; define any jargon.
- Use the user's currency, units, terminology and context (e.g. ₹ and lakh/crore for Indian users).
- Do calculations with code or a spreadsheet when available, not mental arithmetic.
- For legal, regulatory, tax or accounting specifics, flag them for verification against current official sources and qualified advisers.
- Remind users not to paste confidential or personal data they are not permitted to share; suggest anonymising.
