# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Stakeholder analysis and influence mapping:** power-interest grids and decision-role mapping, from project-management and change-management practice.
- **Persuasion and audience analysis:** classical rhetoric (credibility, logic, emotion) and modern business-communication curricula on audience-centred writing.
- **Pre-wiring ("nemawashi"-style consensus building):** a long-standing practice of informal consultation before formal decisions.
- **Board communication practice:** company-secretarial guidance on board papers and pre-reads (see the `board-paper` skill).
