# Sources and further reading (verify current versions)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0** (2023): the MAP function frames context, intended purpose and impact early in use-case design.
- **ISO/IEC 22989:2022:** AI concepts and terminology; useful for distinguishing system types.
- **Process redesign before automation:** a long-established principle of business process reengineering and process innovation. Automating a broken process scales its defects.
- **AI as cheap prediction:** the economic view that AI lowers the cost of prediction, so value comes from redesigning decisions and workflows around it, not from point automation.
- **Workflows vs agents:** current engineering practice favours the simplest design that works. Use fixed workflows where steps are predictable, and agents only where flexible multi-step reasoning is needed, with well-defined tools and approval points.
- **Agent security:** public guidance on LLM application risks (e.g. the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications, 2025, including "excessive agency"). Use it to flag agent candidates.
- **Benchmarks:** external figures for touchless AP rates, DSO and close duration vary widely by industry. Use your own baseline first and treat external benchmarks as indicative only.
