# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Source evaluation:** information-literacy criteria (currency, relevance, authority, accuracy, purpose) taught in research curricula.
- **Evidence hierarchies:** primary and official data, audited filings and peer-reviewed research ranked above secondary summaries and opinion.
- **Statistical literacy:** sample size and selection, response bias, definitions, base rates.
- **Fact-checking practice:** tracing claims to their origin; lateral reading (checking what other independent sources say).
- **AI-generated content:** verify citations independently; generative models can produce plausible but non-existent references.
