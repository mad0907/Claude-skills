# Sources (widely taught concepts; verify any benchmarks before use)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Customer lifetime value and cohort analysis:** standard marketing-analytics and corporate-finance methods (discounted contribution over a finite horizon; retention curves by cohort).
- **CAC, LTV:CAC and payback:** widely used in venture investing and subscription businesses. Rules of thumb (e.g. LTV:CAC ≥3, payback under 12-18 months) are context-dependent indicators, not standards.
- **Contribution margin and break-even analysis:** management-accounting fundamentals.
- **Lending unit economics:** expected loss = probability of default × loss given default × exposure at default, as used in credit-risk practice and Ind AS 109 / IFRS 9 expected-credit-loss accounting.
- **Attribution:** multi-touch and incrementality-testing approaches have known limits. Prefer holdout or geo experiments for major channel decisions.
