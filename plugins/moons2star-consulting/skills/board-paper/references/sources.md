# Sources and what to verify

Primary sources the method draws on. **Laws, circulars and thresholds change, so confirm current versions with the Company Secretary or counsel before relying on any specific rule.**

## India
- **Companies Act, 2013:** s.166 (duties of directors), s.173-174 (meetings, quorum), s.179(3) (powers to be exercised at meetings), s.180 (restrictions on board powers), s.184 (disclosure of interest), s.186 (loans and investments), s.188 (related-party transactions), s.134(5) (directors' responsibility statement), Schedule IV (Code for Independent Directors).
- **Secretarial Standard on Meetings of the Board of Directors (SS-1), ICSI:** notice, agenda and notes on agenda (circulation normally at least seven days before the meeting; conditions for shorter notice), minutes.
- **SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015:** Reg 17 (board composition and information to be placed before the board, Schedule II Part A), Reg 18 (audit committee), Reg 21 (risk management committee), Reg 23 (related-party transactions: verify the current materiality thresholds), Reg 30 (disclosure of material events).
- **SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015:** UPSI handling, structured digital database.
- **SEBI Guidance Note on Board Evaluation** (January 2017): useful for what boards expect in information quality.

## Global practice
- **UK Corporate Governance Code 2024** (Financial Reporting Council), with the FRC's Guidance on Board Effectiveness: board information quality and timeliness. Provision 29 (declaration on material internal controls) applies to periods from 1 January 2026.
- **G20/OECD Principles of Corporate Governance** (2023 revision).

## Further reading
- Professional-body guidance on writing board papers (e.g. ICSI in India).
- Answer-first, pyramid-style structuring of arguments.

## Verify each time
- Reg 23 materiality thresholds and any recent SEBI amendments on RPTs.
- Reg 30 timelines and the materiality criteria for the specific event.
- Sector regulator (RBI / IRDAI / SEBI-intermediary) board-approval requirements for the specific matter.
- Whether an item may be passed by circular resolution.
