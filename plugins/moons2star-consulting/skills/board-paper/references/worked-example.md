# Worked example: Board paper (illustrative; company and numbers are fictional)

# Approval of Phase 1 investment in a new specialty-chemicals plant at Dahej
| Meeting | Item | Purpose | Sponsor | Author | Classification | Previous references |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Board, 14 Feb 2027 | 6 | **For decision** | MD & CEO | Head of Strategy | Confidential - contains UPSI | Strategy 2030 (Board, Jul 2026, Item 4); Capex pipeline (Board, Nov 2026, Item 7) |

## 1. Executive summary
**Ask:** Approve ₹420 crore for Phase 1 of a 30 KTPA specialty-chemicals plant at Dahej. ₹160 crore is to be released now; ₹260 crore is released in two tranches against milestones and a demand check (Annex B). Commissioning is planned for Q3 FY29.

**Why now:** our existing Ankleshwar capacity will run at 96% utilisation from FY28, and two anchor customers have signed letters of intent for 11 KTPA. A 12-month delay pushes commissioning past the window in which these customers qualify suppliers (est. revenue at risk ₹180-220 crore a year).

**Recommendation:** proceed with a phased greenfield build (Option A). It gives the highest NPV (₹212 crore at 12.5% WACC; IRR 18.4% vs 15% hurdle). Phasing caps the irreversible commitment at ₹160 crore until demand is confirmed.

**Key risks (residual):**
- Demand shortfall: medium, mitigated by the tranche gate.
- Capex overrun: medium; EPC lump-sum for 70% of scope.
- Environmental clearance timing: medium-high, and the critical path.

All are within the capital-projects appetite approved in July 2026. **Note:** NPV turns negative if Phase 1 volumes average more than 22% below plan over FY29-33.

**Conflicts and further approvals:** none of the directors is interested. No shareholder approval is required (within s.180 limits). Lenders' consent is needed under the existing term-loan covenant. Disclosure under Reg 30 follows the Board decision.

## 2. Strategic context
Strategy 2030 targets 40% of revenue from specialty grades (FY26: 24%). This plant is the largest single contributor (est. +9 percentage points by FY31). It is consistent with the approved capital allocation envelope (Grow: ₹1,100 crore over FY27-30, of which ₹310 crore is already committed).

## 3. Options considered
| Criterion | A. Phased greenfield, Dahej (recommended) | B. Brownfield debottleneck, Ankleshwar | C. Toll manufacturing | D. Defer 12 months |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Added capacity | 30 KTPA (two phases) | 6 KTPA | 8-10 KTPA, contract-dependent | 0 |
| Capex | ₹420 Cr Phase 1 | ₹95 Cr | Nil | Nil |
| NPV @12.5% | ₹212 Cr | ₹64 Cr | ₹38 Cr | -₹40 to -₹70 Cr (lost margin, est.) |
| Control of quality / IP | Full | Full | Low: process IP exposed | Full |
| Reversibility | Partial (tranches) | High | High | High |
| Key weakness | Clearance timing | Does not meet the anchor demand | Customer qualification risk; margin leakage | Anchor customers likely lost |

B is recommended as a **complement** (Board paper planned for May 2027), not as an alternative.

## 4. Financial impact
- Phase 1 capex: ₹420 Cr, including a 10% contingency (₹38 Cr) set on the basis of a ±15% Class-3 estimate.
- Funding: 60% internal accruals, 40% from the existing term-loan facility. Peak net debt/EBITDA is 1.9x vs a 2.75x covenant and our policy ceiling of 2.25x.
- Returns: NPV ₹212 Cr; IRR 18.4%; payback 5.8 years from first spend.
- Sensitivity (NPV, ₹ Cr):

| Change | NPV |
|---|---|
| Volume -10% | 141 |
| Volume -22% | ~0 (break-even) |
| Capex +20% | 141 |
| Selling price -5% | 118 |
| 9-month delay | 168 |

## 5. Risks
| Risk (cause - event - consequence) | Inherent | Mitigation (owner) | Residual | Early-warning indicator |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer qualification takes longer → volumes ramp more slowly → NPV erosion | High | LOIs converted to supply agreements with take-or-pay for 60% of volume before Tranche 2 (CCO) | Medium | Signed agreements by Sep 2027 |
| Clearance delayed → commissioning slips | High | Pre-filing engagement started; consultants appointed; site-alternative study done (Head Projects) | Medium-high | Public hearing date fixed by Jun 2027 |
| Capex overrun | Medium | Lump-sum EPC for 70% of scope; owner's engineer; monthly cost report to Audit Committee (CFO) | Medium | Cost-to-complete variance >5% |
| Management bandwidth (3 major projects concurrent) | Medium | Dedicated project director hired; PMO (COO) | Low-medium | Milestone slippage >6 weeks |
| Effluent / community incident at new site | Medium | Zero-liquid-discharge design; community engagement plan (Head EHS) | Low | Grievance log; consent compliance |

## 6. Legal, regulatory, governance and stakeholder considerations
- Environmental clearance, consent to establish, and GIDC land allotment (in hand).
- Lender consent under clause 14.3 of the term-loan agreement.
- Reg 30 disclosure of capacity addition after this decision; the item is treated as UPSI until disclosed.
- No related-party aspects; the EPC tender was competitive (4 bidders, Annex D).
- ESG: the plant increases absolute Scope 1-2 emissions by ~4% but reduces intensity by ~6%. The BRSR Core impact is included in the FY29 plan.

## 7. Implementation, stage gates and board monitoring
| Gate | Date | Criteria to release next tranche | Decision by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tranche 2 (₹170 Cr) | Q4 FY28 | Environmental clearance received; ≥60% Phase 1 volume under take-or-pay; cost-to-complete within +5% | Board |
| Tranche 3 (₹90 Cr) | Q2 FY29 | Mechanical completion ≥70%; qualification batches passed for 2 anchors | Investment Committee |

Quarterly reporting to the Board: milestones, cost-to-complete, contracted volumes, and risk indicators.

**Kill criterion:** if take-or-pay contracts cover <40% of Phase 1 volume by Q4 FY28, management will return with options (pause, resize or sell the site) before any further release.

## 8. Recommendation and draft resolution
> RESOLVED THAT approval of the Board be and is hereby accorded for Phase 1 of the specialty-chemicals plant at Dahej at a total cost not exceeding ₹420 crore (including contingency of ₹38 crore), to be released in three tranches as per the stage gates set out in Annex B to the note placed before the Board, with an initial release of ₹160 crore.
>
> FURTHER RESOLVED THAT the release of Tranche 2 shall require the approval of the Board, and Tranche 3 the approval of the Investment Committee, in each case on satisfaction of the criteria in Annex B.
>
> FURTHER RESOLVED THAT the MD & CEO and CFO be and are hereby severally authorised to finalise and execute the EPC contract and related agreements within the approved cost, and to seek the lenders' consent, provided that any increase above 5% of the approved cost shall be placed before the Board, this authority being valid until 31 March 2029.
