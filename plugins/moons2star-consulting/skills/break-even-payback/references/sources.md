# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Cost-volume-profit (CVP) analysis, contribution margin, margin of safety, operating leverage:** management-accounting fundamentals (e.g. the cost and management accounting syllabi of professional accounting bodies).
- **Payback and discounted payback:** capital-budgeting basics; use alongside NPV.
- **Working-capital cycle:** receivable, inventory and payable days, from standard corporate-finance practice.
- **Benchmarks:** use your own comparable sites or pilots first; external industry ratios vary by city tier, format and payer mix.
