# Sources (widely taught concepts and public data)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Five forces framework of industry analysis:** rivalry, entry, buyer power, supplier power, substitutes. A core strategy-curriculum framework, with later extensions on complements and the role of government.
- **Industry profitability data:** company filings; industry-association reports; capital-market data on returns by sector.
- **Platform economics:** network effects, multi-homing, aggregator power (strategy and economics curricula).
- **Competition and sector regulation:** Competition Act, 2002 (India) and sector regulators, as shaping factors.
