# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **SWOT analysis:** a long-standing strategic-planning tool taught in strategy curricula; criticised when used without evidence or follow-through, which is why this skill insists on evidence and TOWS.
- **TOWS matrix:** the extension that systematically pairs internal and external factors to generate strategies (SO, ST, WO, WT), widely taught in strategy courses.
- **Resource-based view of the firm:** strengths should be valuable, rare and hard to imitate to be strategic (see the `vrio-analysis` skill).
- **External analysis:** PESTEL and Five Forces (see the related skills) as inputs for opportunities and threats.
