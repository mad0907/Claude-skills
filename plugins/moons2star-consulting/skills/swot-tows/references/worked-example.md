# Worked example (illustrative; fictional regional hospital chain, 6 hospitals in 3 Tier-2 cities)

**Question:** How do we defend and grow cardiac and oncology revenue in our 3 cities as two national chains open hospitals between 2027 and 2029?
**Comparison set:** the national chains' new hospitals, 2 local trust hospitals, and patients travelling to metros.

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|
| 1. Highest cardiac procedure volume in 2 of 3 districts (state insurance scheme data: 41% share) | 1. No PET-CT or radiation oncology; 30% of oncology patients referred out |
| 2. 140 referring physicians with 10+ year relationships (CRM: 62% of IP admissions via referrals) | 2. ARPOB 18% below national chains (payer mix skewed to government schemes) |
| 3. Cost per bed-day ~22% below national-chain benchmarks (published investor data) | 3. Senior consultant attrition 19% (2 key cardiologists left this year) |
| 4. Trusted local brand: NPS 61 vs 48 for the trust hospital (own survey, n=1,200) | 4. Limited digital front door; 8% of appointments booked online |
| **Opportunities** | **Threats** |
| 1. Oncology demand growing ~12%/yr; current metro travel for radiation (high impact, now) | 1. National chain A opens 300 beds in City 1 in 2027 with a cardiac focus (high impact, likely) |
| 2. Insurance penetration rising in Tier-2 cities (medium impact, 2-4 yrs) | 2. Poaching of cardiologists with 30-40% pay premiums (high, likely) |
| 3. State scheme package-rate revisions for cardiac procedures (medium, 1-2 yrs; verify) | 3. Government scheme payment delays (avg 95 days) squeeze cash (medium, ongoing) |
| 4. Tele-consult and remote monitoring for chronic cardiac patients (medium) | 4. Rising compliance and quality-accreditation costs (medium) |

## TOWS options
| | Opportunities | Threats |
|---|---|---|
| **S** | **SO1:** Build a cardiac centre of excellence with a referral-physician programme (joint clinics, tele-ECG) to grow volumes 20% before Chain A opens | **ST1:** Lock in key cardiologists with equity-like retention (revenue share + academic roles) |
| **W** | **WO1:** Partner with an oncology specialist for radiation oncology and PET-CT under revenue share (avoids ₹60 Cr capex) | **WT1:** Reduce dependence on government schemes in City 3: renegotiate empanelment mix; factor receivables |
| **W** | **WO2:** Digital front door: booking, reports, payments; target 35% online bookings | **WT2:** Exit low-margin general surgery in City 2 where Chain A will compete; redeploy beds to day care |

## Priority options
| Option | Impact | Feasibility | Time | Risk | What must be true | First step / owner |
