# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Answer-first executive writing:** pyramid-style structure and "bottom line up front", as taught in business-communication curricula.
- **Plain language:** ISO 24495-1:2023 and public plain-language guidelines (short sentences, active voice, concrete numbers).
- **Board and committee paper conventions:** company-secretarial guidance (see the `board-paper` skill).
- **Investment-committee memo practice:** returns vs hurdle, risks, conditions (see the `business-case` skill).
