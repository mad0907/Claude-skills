# Worked example (illustrative; fictional bank, core-banking vendor assessment, 30-page report)

## Executive Summary: core banking platform selection
**Recommendation and ask:** select Vendor B for the core banking upgrade, and approve ₹62 Cr (7-year total cost: ₹148 Cr) by 15 December so contracting can complete before the current vendor's support ends in March 2028.

**Why:**
- **Lowest 7-year total cost:** ₹148 Cr vs ₹162 Cr (Vendor A) and ₹171 Cr (Vendor C), including licences, implementation, infrastructure and run costs (Finance-validated model, Annex 3).
- **Proven at our scale in India:** live at 3 banks of similar size. Reference calls confirmed on-time go-lives and stable month-end processing (Annex 5).
- **Fastest migration:** an 18-month plan vs 27 months for A, so the old platform is retired 9 months sooner (saving ₹6 Cr of dual running).
- **Strongest regulatory fit:** native support for current RBI reporting formats and data localisation. The other two need customisation.

**Main risk / trade-off:** Vendor B's local support team is smaller (40 vs 120 at A).
→ **Mitigation:** contracted service levels with financial penalties; a dedicated on-site team of 8 during migration; escrow of the source code.

**Next steps:**
- CIO: complete negotiations by 30 Nov.
- General Counsel: contract and escrow terms by 10 Dec.
- Board approval on 15 Dec.
- Programme mobilisation from January.
