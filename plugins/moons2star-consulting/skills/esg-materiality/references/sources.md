# Sources (verify current versions and applicability)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **IFRS S1** (General requirements) and **IFRS S2** (Climate), ISSB, June 2023; SASB Standards (industry topics), now maintained by the ISSB. Check India's adoption roadmap (ICAI / regulators).
- **GRI 1, 2, 3 (2021)**: GRI 3 *Material Topics*; GRI sector standards (e.g. oil and gas, coal, agriculture, mining, financial services as published).
- **ESRS 1** (General requirements, double materiality) and EFRAG Implementation Guidance IG 1 (Materiality assessment). CSRD scope and ESRS content were simplified by the EU Omnibus process (2025-26); verify which companies, and which years, are in scope.
- **UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights** (2011): severity criteria; **OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises on Responsible Business Conduct** (2023 update) and OECD Due Diligence Guidance.
- **TCFD** recommendations (2017; now incorporated into IFRS S2); **TNFD** recommendations (2023) for nature-related dependencies and impacts.
- **India:** SEBI BRSR format and BRSR Core (see `brsr-readiness`); National Guidelines on Responsible Business Conduct (NGRBC, 2019); Carbon Credit Trading Scheme, 2023 and notified GHG emission-intensity targets for covered sectors (verify).
- **EU CBAM** (Regulation (EU) 2023/956): definitive period from 1 Jan 2026, with simplifications adopted in 2025 (verify thresholds and obligations).
