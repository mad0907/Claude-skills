# ESG materiality: scoring scales

## Impact materiality scales (1-5)
| Score | Scale (how grave) | Scope (how widespread) | Irremediability (how hard to reverse) | Likelihood (potential impacts) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Minimal | Very limited (few people, one small site) | Easily remediated, short term | Remote (<5%) |
| 2 | Low | Limited | Remediable with effort | Unlikely |
| 3 | Medium | Moderate (a region, a supplier tier) | Remediable over the long term | Possible |
| 4 | High | Widespread | Difficult to remediate | Likely |
| 5 | Very high (loss of life, irreversible ecosystem damage) | Global or systemic | Irreversible | Very likely / actual |

- Severity = average (or max-weighted) of scale, scope and irremediability.
- **Potential** negative impacts: severity × likelihood, but human-rights impacts with severity ≥4 are treated as material regardless of likelihood.

## Financial materiality scale (1-5)
| Score | Magnitude (illustrative; calibrate to the company) | Likelihood |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | <0.5% EBITDA or negligible effect on capital access | Remote |
| 2 | 0.5-2% EBITDA | Unlikely |
| 3 | 2-5% EBITDA, or a covenant / rating watch | Possible |
| 4 | 5-10% EBITDA, or loss of a key customer or market | Likely |
| 5 | >10% EBITDA, a licence to operate, or a major refinancing risk | Very likely |

Score separately for short, medium and long term. A topic can be immaterial now and material by 2030; that is when strategy needs to act.
