# Sources and what to verify

- **Companies Act, 2013:** s.166 (duties of directors, including acting in the interests of the company, its employees, shareholders, the community and the environment); s.134(5) (directors' responsibility statement, internal financial controls); s.177 (audit committee; vigil mechanism); Schedule IV (Code for Independent Directors: role, duties, separate meetings).
- **SEBI LODR Regulations, 2015:** Reg 17 and Schedule II (information to be placed before the board), Reg 18 (audit committee role, Part C of Schedule II), Reg 21 (risk management committee), Reg 23 (related-party transactions), Reg 25 (independent directors).
- **SEBI Guidance Note on Board Evaluation** (Jan 2017).
- **G20/OECD Principles of Corporate Governance** (2023).
- **UK Corporate Governance Code 2024** and **FRC Guidance on Board Effectiveness**.
- **Question-based oversight:** the widely used practice of structured director question sets by topic (strategy, risk, technology, culture).
- **CERT-In Directions** (April 2022): incident reporting obligations in India (verify current timelines).
- **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** and **DPDP Rules, 2025** (notified 13 Nov 2025; most data-fiduciary obligations apply from 13 May 2027; verify).
- **RBI FREE-AI Committee report** (Aug 2025) and **MeitY India AI Governance Guidelines** (Nov 2025): for AI questions in Indian financial services and generally.

**Verify each time:** sector-regulator expectations (RBI / IRDAI / SEBI) on board oversight of the specific topic, and any recent circulars.
