# Worked example (illustrative; fictional mid-size Indian private bank vs 4 peers, MSME and retail focus, FY26)

**Competitive set:**
- **Direct:** Banks A, B, C (mid-size private).
- **Adjacent:** small finance bank D.
- **Substitutes / new entrants:** fintech lenders (digital MSME credit), represented by a composite.

**Choice drivers (MSME customers, from 60 interviews):** speed of credit decision (30%), relationship-manager quality (20%), pricing (20%), digital banking (15%), product breadth (15%).

| Criterion | Us | Bank A | Bank B | Bank C | SFB D | Fintech composite | Source / date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MSME loan decision time (≤₹50 L) | 9 days | 7 | 12 | 6 | 4 | 2 days | Mystery shopping, Q1 FY27 |
| Cost-to-income | 54% | 47% | 51% | 45% | 58% | — | Annual reports FY26 |
| CASA ratio | 34% | 38% | 31% | 41% | 22% | — | Annual reports FY26 |
| Digital MSME onboarding (fully online) | No | Partial | No | Yes | Yes | Yes | Websites, Q1 FY27 |
| Gross NPA (MSME) | 3.9% | 3.1% | 4.6% | 2.8% | 4.2% | n/a | Disclosures FY26 |
| Customer NPS (MSME) | 31 | 35 | 22 | 38 | 29 | 44 | Survey, n=600 (est.) |

**Positioning map** (speed of credit vs relationship depth):
- Fintechs: fast and thin.
- Banks A and C: fast-ish and deep.
- **We sit mid-slow and deep.**
- White space: "fast and deep" for MSMEs needing ₹20 L-₹2 Cr.

**Likely next moves:**
- Bank C is expanding its digital MSME lending to Tier-2 cities (job postings, product launches).
- Fintechs are partnering with banks for co-lending (regulatory filings).

**Implications:**
- **Lag:** decision speed and cost-to-income. Fix with straight-through processing for loans under ₹50 L, targeting ≤3 days.
- **Lead:** relationship depth in Tier-2 clusters. Defend it with an RM-plus-digital model.
- **White space:** fast and deep MSME credit of ₹20 L-₹2 Cr.
- **Pre-empt:** consider co-lending with a fintech to match speed while using our balance sheet.
