# Sources (public data types and laws; verify current versions)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Competitive analysis methods:** competitor profiling, positioning maps and win/loss analysis, from strategy and marketing curricula.
- **Public information:** annual reports, investor presentations, regulatory disclosures (e.g. bank Basel III Pillar 3 disclosures), websites, pricing pages, job postings, product release notes, customer reviews.
- **Competitive-intelligence ethics:** professional codes requiring lawful, ethical collection and the avoidance of misrepresentation.
- **Competition law:** India's Competition Act, 2002 (anti-competitive agreements; information exchange among competitors) and equivalents elsewhere. Avoid exchanging competitively sensitive information with rivals.
