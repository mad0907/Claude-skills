# Worked example (illustrative; fictional regional bank with a sub-scale retail-banking operation in a neighbouring country)

**Key question:** what should we do with our retail-banking business in Country X (42 branches, 3% market share, ROE 6% vs 13% cost of equity)?

**Governing hypothesis:** we should exit retail banking in Country X via a sale to a local player within 18 months, because a credible plan cannot lift ROE above the cost of equity within 3 years, and a buyer will pay at least book value.

| # | Claim | Test | Data | Confirms if | Kills if | F×C | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Standalone ROE stays < 13% through FY29 even with a cost plan | Bottom-up turnaround model (cost, pricing, digital migration) | Finance, benchmarks | Best credible case ROE < 11% | Credible plan reaches ≥13% by FY29 | High × High → **first** | CFO | Testing |
| 2 | A buyer will pay ≥1.0x book | Confidential soundings with 4-6 potential buyers; transaction comparables | Advisers, deal data | ≥2 indications ≥1.0x | All indications < 0.8x | High × High | Strategy | Not started |
| 3 | Exit doesn't damage group strategy (corporate clients, trade finance) | Revenue linkage analysis; client interviews | CRM, interviews | <5% of group revenue at risk | >10% at risk | Medium × High | Head Corporate | Not started |
| 4 | Regulatory approval is obtainable within 12 months | Counsel view; regulator precedent | Legal | Clear precedent | Material conditions or delay > 18 months | Medium × Medium | General Counsel | Not started |
| 5 | Separation costs are < 15% of proceeds | IT and operations separation estimate | IT, Operations | < 15% | > 25% | Medium × Medium | COO | Not started |

**Disconfirmation tests:**
- For claim 1, the turnaround is modelled by a team **not** involved in the exit recommendation, and the best-performing peer is benchmarked to see what's possible.
- For claim 3, interview the 20 largest corporate clients specifically about the risk of losing retail services.

**Alternative hypothesis:** keep the business and refocus on affluent customers and digital channels (close 25 branches), because the affluent segment earns 18% ROE.
*Evidence that would favour it:* segment-level ROE confirming ≥15% for affluent customers, and digital adoption ≥40%.

**What would change our mind:**
- A credible plan to ≥13% ROE.
- Buyer indications < 0.8x book.
- A strategic dependency of corporate clients on retail.
