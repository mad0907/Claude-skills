# BRSR and BRSR Core Readiness

*moons2star Consultant's Toolkit · created by Dr. Munmun Das · [moons2star.in](https://moons2star.in)*

**What this gives you:** a clear view of whether the company can produce a complete, accurate and assessment- or assurance-ready BRSR on time. It shows which numbers would fail a provider's testing and why, who owns each fix, and what the board must oversee.

> **Read first:** BRSR requirements are set by SEBI circulars and the LODR Master Circular, and they have changed repeatedly (2021, 2023, 2024-25). Everything below reflects research as of November 2026. **Confirm applicability, glide-path dates and the current format against the latest SEBI circulars with your Company Secretary and assurance provider before relying on it.**

## Regulatory frame (summary; verify)
- **BRSR:** mandatory for the **top 1,000 listed entities by market capitalisation** as part of the annual report (since FY 2022-23), structured around the **nine principles of the National Guidelines on Responsible Business Conduct (NGRBC)**.
  - **Section A:** general disclosures.
  - **Section B:** management and process.
  - **Section C:** principle-wise performance, with **essential** (mandatory) and **leadership** (voluntary) indicators.
- **BRSR Core** (SEBI circular of 12 July 2023, amended by the circular of 28 March 2025): a subset of KPIs under **nine ESG attributes**.
  - Coverage was phased by market-cap rank: top 150 (FY 2023-24), top 250 (FY 2024-25), top 500 (FY 2025-26), top 1,000 (FY 2026-27).
  - The 2025 amendment changed "reasonable assurance" to **"assessment or assurance"**. Assessment follows standards developed by the Industry Standards Forum in consultation with SEBI.
  - Intensity ratios are computed on **revenue adjusted for purchasing power parity (PPP)**.
  - Additional green-credit and similar disclosures were introduced; verify.
- **Value-chain disclosures** (per the 2025 amendment, as reported): top 250 entities.
  - Value-chain partners are those individually ≥2% of purchases or sales by value; disclosure covers partners making up 75% of purchases and of sales.
  - Disclosure is **voluntary from FY 2025-26**, with assessment or assurance **voluntary from FY 2026-27**.
  - Prior-year comparatives are optional in the first year.
- **Provider independence:** the board must ensure the assessment or assurance provider has the necessary expertise. The provider and its associates must not provide non-audit, non-assurance consulting services to the entity or its group (as reported; verify the exact conditions).

## The nine BRSR Core attributes (verify the current KPI list)
1. GHG footprint (Scope 1, Scope 2; intensity)
2. Water footprint (withdrawal, consumption, discharge; intensity)
3. Energy footprint (consumption, renewable share; intensity)
4. Embracing circularity: waste generated, recovered and disposed (by category; intensity)
5. Employee well-being and safety (spend on well-being; safety incidents, LTIFR, fatalities)
6. Gender diversity in business (wages paid to women; POSH complaints)
7. Inclusive development (input material sourced from MSMEs and small producers and from within India; job creation in smaller towns)
8. Fairness in engaging with customers and suppliers (cyber and data-breach instances; days of accounts payable)
9. Openness of business (concentration of purchases and sales with trading houses, dealers and distributors; related-party share of purchases, sales, loans, advances and investments)

## Method
1. **Applicability and timeline:** market-cap rank (as at the relevant date), BRSR Core tier for the year, value-chain status, filing date (with the annual report), and the assessment or assurance provider's engagement timeline (start testing at least 4-6 months before year-end).
2. **Indicator inventory:** list every indicator in Sections A, B and C plus BRSR Core KPIs. For each, record the **data owner, source system, calculation method / methodology reference, boundary (entities, sites), frequency and evidence**.
3. **Data-trail test (the assurance lens):** for each Core KPI, can a provider trace from the reported number → calculation → source records → original evidence (meter readings, invoices, manifests, HR records, incident registers)? Rate each KPI:
   - **Ready:** system-sourced, reconciled, controls evidenced.
   - **Partial:** spreadsheet-based but traceable; reconciliations missing.
   - **Not ready:** estimates, missing sites, undocumented methods.
4. **Boundary and consistency checks:**
   - Are all subsidiaries and sites in the BRSR boundary covered, and consistent with the financial-reporting boundary where required?
   - Are restatements explained?
   - Are units consistent (kWh vs GJ; kL vs m³)?
5. **Methodology checks:**
   - **GHG:** the GHG Protocol is the usual basis. Emission factors are documented and dated (e.g. India's CEA grid emission factor for Scope 2). Market vs location-based Scope 2 is stated.
   - **Water:** withdrawal vs consumption vs discharge definitions; water-stressed area identification.
   - **Waste:** hazardous categories aligned with the relevant rules; recovery vs disposal evidence (manifests, recycler certificates).
   - **Safety:** LTIFR formula and denominator (per million person-hours worked); contractor coverage.
   - **PPP revenue:** the source and year of the PPP conversion factor (e.g. the IMF / World Bank figure as specified).
6. **Controls:** design minimum controls: maker-checker on data entry; monthly or quarterly reconciliations (e.g. fuel purchase invoices vs consumption logs); site sign-offs; a change log for methodologies; IT controls on the ESG data platform; internal-audit coverage.
7. **Value chain (if in scope or chosen voluntarily):** identify partners above the 2% thresholds up to 75% coverage; design a data request; set data-quality expectations; plan supplier capacity building.
8. **Governance:**
   - Board or committee oversight (a CSR/ESG or risk committee).
   - Approval of the BRSR.
   - The **director responsible** for BRSR (Section B).
   - Provider appointment and independence check.
   - Management representation letters.
9. **Gap closing plan:** prioritise Core KPIs rated Not ready or Partial; assign owners and dates; plan a dry run (Q3 data) with the provider to surface issues before year-end.

## Judgement calls
- **Assurance readiness is about evidence, not numbers.** A correct number with no trail fails; an approximate number with a documented, consistent method and evidence often passes, with qualification.
- **Start with the 9 Core attributes,** then fill BRSR gaps. Core failures carry the most reputational risk.
- **Sites are where data breaks.** Contractor hours, diesel in gensets, water from tankers and waste via informal recyclers are typical blind spots.
- **Don't let the consultant own the numbers.** Management must own the data and methods; independence rules may also restrict who can help.
- **Use BRSR as an operating tool.** Intensity KPIs belong in plant dashboards and the risk appetite, not only in the annual report.
