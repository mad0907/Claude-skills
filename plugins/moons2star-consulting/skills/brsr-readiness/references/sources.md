# Sources (as researched November 2026; verify against SEBI's website)

- **SEBI circular SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-SEC-2/P/CIR/2023/122 (12 July 2023):** BRSR Core framework for assurance and ESG disclosures for the value chain; Annexure I (BRSR Core format), Annexure II (updated BRSR format). Original glide path of reasonable assurance: top 150 (FY 2023-24), top 250 (FY 2024-25), top 500 (FY 2025-26), top 1,000 (FY 2026-27).
- **SEBI circular of 28 March 2025:** amended the BRSR Core framework (as reported):
  - "assessment or assurance" in place of "reasonable assurance";
  - assessment standards by the Industry Standards Forum in consultation with SEBI;
  - value-chain disclosures voluntary for the top 250 from FY 2025-26, with assessment or assurance voluntary from FY 2026-27;
  - the 2% / 75% value-chain thresholds;
  - provider independence conditions.
- **SEBI LODR Master Circular** (latest): consolidated BRSR requirements; Reg 34(2)(f) LODR (BRSR in the annual report for the top 1,000).
- **National Guidelines on Responsible Business Conduct (NGRBC)**, Ministry of Corporate Affairs, 2019: the nine principles.
- **GHG Protocol** Corporate Standard and Scope 2 Guidance; **Central Electricity Authority (CEA)** CO2 baseline database for the Indian power sector (grid emission factor; use the current version).
- **ICAI Sustainability Reporting Standards Board** guidance / standards on sustainability assurance (e.g. SSAE 3000, SSAE 3410 for GHG; verify current numbering and status).
- **Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Rules, 2016;** Plastic Waste Management Rules (EPR); Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (POSH) Act, 2013; MSMED Act and Udyam registration.

**Note:** secondary summaries (consultancies, ESG platforms) were used to cross-check the 2025 amendment. Read the primary SEBI circular before relying on any detail.
