# Sources and further reading (verify current versions)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Capital budgeting:** NPV, IRR and payback as taught in standard corporate-finance curricula. Use the company's own hurdle rate, horizon and capitalisation policies.
- **Benefits realisation:** public-sector project-appraisal and benefits-management guidance (e.g. UK HM Treasury *Green Book* and related benefits-management guidance) and professional project-management bodies' practice standards.
- **Evidence on GenAI productivity:** peer-reviewed and working-paper field studies (2023-2025) of GenAI use in customer support, consulting-style tasks and software development. They consistently find large but uneven gains: strongest on routine tasks and for less-experienced workers, with quality drops on tasks outside the model's competence. This supports modelling task mix, adoption ramps and rework rather than a single percentage.
- **Inference cost drivers:** model providers' published per-token input and output prices and caching discounts. Prices change frequently, so use current rates and keep a ±50% sensitivity.
- **NIST AI RMF 1.0** and **ISO/IEC 42001:2023:** governance activities whose cost should be included.
- **Agentic systems:** agent loops multiply calls and tokens. Measure cost per completed task in the pilot, not cost per call.
