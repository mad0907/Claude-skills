# Sources (widely taught concepts; verify regulatory specifics)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Make-or-buy and transaction-cost reasoning:** asset specificity, uncertainty and frequency as drivers of integrating vs contracting (economics and strategy curricula).
- **Resources and capabilities pathways:** choosing between internal development, contracts and licensing, alliances, and acquisitions according to resource relevance and the organisation's ability to integrate (strategy curricula).
- **M&A integration practice:** synergy tracking, integration management offices, retention, cultural integration.
- **Outsourcing regulation (India; verify current versions):** RBI directions on managing risks in outsourcing (financial and IT services) for regulated entities; IRDAI outsourcing regulations for insurers; DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025 on processors and cross-border transfer.
- **Technology contracts:** standard practice on IP ownership, data rights, service levels, audit, step-in, and termination assistance.
- **AI-specific risks:** NIST AI RMF third-party risk considerations; ISO/IEC 42001 supplier controls.
