# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Return-on-investment decomposition** (return = margin × asset turnover, extended into profitability trees): a classic management-accounting and corporate-finance technique.
- **Value-based management and economic profit** (operating profit − capital charge): corporate-finance curricula.
- **Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)** = availability × performance × quality: standard in manufacturing and lean practice; see ISO 22400 (KPIs for manufacturing operations management).
- **Leading vs lagging indicators:** standard performance-management practice, including balanced-scorecard-style approaches.
- **Sector metrics** (e.g. specific heat consumption in cement, NIM in banking, ARPOB in hospitals): use industry-association definitions and your regulator's reporting definitions for consistency.
