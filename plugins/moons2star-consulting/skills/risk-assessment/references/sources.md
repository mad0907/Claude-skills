# Sources (public standards; verify sector specifics)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **ISO 31000:2018:** risk management principles, framework and process (risk = effect of uncertainty on objectives).
- **ISO 31010:2019:** risk assessment techniques (e.g. bow-tie, scenario analysis, FMEA, checklists).
- **COSO Enterprise Risk Management: Integrating with Strategy and Performance (2017).**
- **India:** Companies Act 2013 (s.134(3)(n) risk management policy statement; s.177 audit committee evaluation of risk management); SEBI LODR Reg 17(9) and Reg 21 (risk management committee for the top 1,000 listed entities); sector regulators' risk-management directions (RBI, IRDAI, SEBI-intermediaries).
- **Operational resilience:** Basel Committee principles (2021); RBI guidance note on operational risk management and operational resilience (2024).
