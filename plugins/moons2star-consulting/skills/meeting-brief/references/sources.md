# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Stakeholder analysis:** interests vs positions; influence-interest mapping, as taught in management and negotiation curricula.
- **Message discipline:** a few key messages with proof points; question anticipation and bridging, as used in executive communication training.
- **Pre-wiring:** socialising proposals with key stakeholders before formal decision forums.
- **Disclosure rules:** listed-company obligations on price-sensitive information and fair disclosure (e.g. SEBI LODR and PIT Regulations in India).
- **Public procurement:** rules governing engagement with government buyers (state and central procurement rules).
