# Sources (widely taught concepts and public data)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Value chain analysis:** primary and support activities as sources of competitive advantage; industry value-system analysis. Core strategy-curriculum concepts.
- **Profit-pool mapping:** revenue × margin by value-chain step to locate where profits are earned.
- **Make-or-buy and vertical integration:** transaction-cost and capability considerations (see `build-buy-partner`).
- **Sector data (India; verify current rules):** trade-margin regulation and price controls for pharmaceuticals (national pharmaceutical pricing authority orders); company filings; industry-association statistics.
