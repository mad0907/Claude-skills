# Worked example (illustrative; branded generics pharma in India, industry lens, % of maximum retail price (MRP))

| Step | Activities | Who | Share of MRP | Margin (on own revenue) | Capital | Concentration | Our position | Shift |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| API / key starting materials | Chemical synthesis | API makers (domestic + imports) | ~10-12% | 12-18% EBITDA | High | Moderate; import dependence for some | We buy 70% externally | Incentives for domestic APIs; China+1 |
| Formulation manufacturing | Tablets, capsules, injectables | Branded companies + contract manufacturers | ~15-18% (cost) | — | Medium-high | Fragmented contract manufacturing | Own 3 plants; cost per unit ~8% above best peer | Tighter GMP rules raise compliance costs |
| Brand and marketing | Medical representatives, brand building | Branded companies | ~20-25% (incl. field force) | Brand EBITDA ~20-25% (est.) | Low | Top 10 hold ~45% share | Strong in 2 therapies (cardiac, diabetes) | Digital detailing; trade-generics growth |
| Distribution | Stockists, distributors | Wholesale network | ~8-10% (trade margin) | 2-4% | Working capital | Consolidating | Standard terms | Digital B2B distribution platforms |
| Retail pharmacy | Dispensing | Chemists, chains, e-pharmacy | ~16-20% (trade margin) | 4-8% | Medium | Fragmented; chains growing | — | Chains and e-pharmacy gain bargaining power |

(Shares are illustrative; verify against current trade-margin rules and company data.)

**Profit pools:** brand ownership (marketing-led) earns the highest returns on capital. Distribution is high-revenue but low-margin. API is attractive under incentives but capital-heavy.

**Our advantage and disadvantage:**
- **Advantage:** prescriber relationships in cardiac and diabetes (market share 2x our overall average).
- **Disadvantage:** manufacturing cost and field-force productivity (prescriptions per medical rep 15% below peers).

**Options:**
- **Improve:** field-force productivity via AI-assisted targeting and digital detailing (+₹30-40 Cr EBITDA est.).
- **Outsource:** move low-volume SKUs to contract manufacturers (capex avoided ₹60 Cr).
- **Integrate (selective):** backward integration into 2 critical APIs under incentives (supply security for top brands).
- **Partner:** with retail chains and e-pharmacy for adherence programmes in chronic therapies.

**Trade-off:** API integration adds capital and complexity. Pursue it only for molecules where supply risk threatens top brands.
