# Worked example (illustrative; quarterly performance deck for an ExCo)

| # | Old title | New action title | Body supports it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Executive summary | Q3 profit is 4% below plan; pricing and two plant issues explain all of it, and both are fixable by Q1 | Yes |
| 2 | Revenue overview | Revenue grew 9%, but all growth came from price; volumes fell 3% | Add a volume-price bridge |
| 3 | Customer survey results | Customers rate us best on quality but worst on delivery reliability, which drives 60% of switching intent | Yes (driver analysis on slide) |
| 4 | Cost analysis | Two plant outages added ₹14 Cr of cost; underlying unit cost is on plan | Split one-offs vs underlying in the chart |
| 5 | Competitor update | A new entrant is undercutting us by 8% in two regions, but has won only 3% share so far | Add a share trend |
| 6 | Market trends | (no clear message: background only) | **Move to appendix** |
| 7 | Initiatives | Delivery reliability programme can recover 2 points of share by Q2 for ₹6 Cr | Needs a sizing exhibit |
| 8 | Next steps | Three decisions today secure ₹20 Cr of FY27 profit | Yes: list the decisions |

**Storyline read:**
1. Q3 profit is 4% below plan, and fixable.
2. Growth was all price; volume fell.
3. Delivery reliability is our weakness and drives switching.
4. The cost overrun is one-off.
5. A new entrant is a contained threat.
6. The delivery programme recovers share.
7. Three decisions today.

**Exhibit changes:** add a volume-price bridge (slide 2); split one-offs (slide 4); add a share trend (slide 5); add a sizing waterfall (slide 7).
