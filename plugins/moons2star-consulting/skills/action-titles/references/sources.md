# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Action titles / "headline" slide titles:** standard practice in consulting and executive presentation training; research on assertion-evidence slide design shows better comprehension when titles state a full-sentence claim supported by visual evidence.
- **Horizontal and vertical logic in decks:** structured-communication practice (see `pyramid-structure` and `scqa-storyline`).
- **Plain language:** ISO 24495-1:2023 principles.
