# Sources (widely taught concepts)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Qualitative analysis:** thematic analysis (coding, theme development, review); deductive and inductive coding; framework matrices, as taught in qualitative-research methods curricula.
- **Saturation:** the point where new data adds no new themes.
- **Rigour:** reporting frequency across respondents, negative cases, reflexivity about researcher bias.
- **Ethics:** anonymisation; small-group suppression in employee research; data protection (DPDP Act 2023 in India).
