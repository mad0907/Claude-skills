# Sources (widely taught concepts and public guidance; verify legal specifics)

*This toolkit references public standards, laws and widely taught concepts only. It names no companies, products or individuals.*

- **Zero-based budgeting:** a long-established budgeting method, rebuilding budgets from activities and needs rather than prior-year baselines.
- **Activity-based costing and cost-to-serve analysis:** management-accounting fundamentals.
- **Spend analysis and category management:** standard procurement practice (spend cube; should-cost modelling; supplier consolidation).
- **Organisation design:** spans of control and layers; the principles of shared-services and global-capability-centre location strategy.
- **Lean process improvement:** waste elimination, standard work, first-time-right.
- **India employment law** (verify with counsel and HR): Industrial Relations Code 2020 and applicable state rules on retrenchment, closure, notice and compensation; Shops and Establishments Acts; contract labour rules. Other jurisdictions have their own consultation and redundancy laws.
- **Accounting for restructuring costs:** Ind AS 37 / IAS 37 provisions; confirm with Finance and auditors.
