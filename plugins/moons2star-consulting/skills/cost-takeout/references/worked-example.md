# Worked example (illustrative; fictional apparel retail chain, ₹500 Cr operating cost base, target ₹40 Cr (8%) in 18 months)

## Baseline (FY, reconciled to P&L; ₹ Cr)
| Category | Cost | % | Controllable? | Key driver | Benchmark gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Store staff | 145 | 29% | Yes | Hours scheduled vs footfall | Hours per ₹ lakh sales 12% above best quartile of own stores |
| Rent and CAM | 120 | 24% | Partly (renewals) | Area, rates | 18 stores above 14% rent-to-sales |

| Technology | 30 | 6% | Yes | Licences, legacy systems | 3 overlapping POS / ERP contracts |

**Pipeline:** ₹35.2 Cr cost run-rate identified (**88% of target**), plus the markdown margin benefit (reported separately). **Gap to a 120% pipeline (₹48 Cr): ~₹13 Cr.** Candidates:
- zero-based review of HQ discretionary spend and travel policy (est. ₹4-5 Cr);
- packaging specification changes (₹2-3 Cr);
- store maintenance and security contract re-tender (₹2 Cr);
- the next wave of rent renewals (₹3-4 Cr).

**Protected:** store experience in the top 40 stores, e-commerce growth marketing, IT security, fire and safety maintenance.

**Banking:** savings are removed from FY budgets on reaching L3. Finance validates monthly. Headcount controls on HQ roles. A 12-month review checks for cost creep.

**Unintended-effect KPIs:** checkout wait time, stock availability, NPS, regretted attrition, like-for-like sales.
